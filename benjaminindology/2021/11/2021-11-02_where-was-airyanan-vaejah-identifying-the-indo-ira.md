The Jambudvipa – Eurasia as known to ancient Indians

According to Zoroastrian tradition, Airyana Vaēǰah is situated at the center of the world. Its eastern boundary is the river Dāityā, and its western boundary is the river Raŋhā. Both rivers are said to drain into a sea called Vourukaša. On the shores of Dāityā were created the first bull and the first man, and Yima and Zoroaster were also born there. The land had a harsh cold weather with ten months of winter.

In order to identify the Airyana Vaēǰah, therefore, we must identify the rivers Dāityā and Raŋhā. The idea of Raŋhā being the westernmost river is found in both Indo-Aryan and Iranian texts. The Iranians later regarded the Tigris or Euphrates as the Raŋhā, since that became their western boundary. In the Rigveda, Rasā is the westernmost tributary of the Sindhu, and the sun is said to set beyond the Rasā. What is common to both traditions, however, is the idea that Raŋhā is the westernmost river of the Indo-Iranians.

The eastern river Dāityā has no equivalent in Rigveda, however, Daityas are a class of demons in Hindu mythology, often said to reside in the far east. The Ural river was called Daikos by Ptolemy. If ‘Daikos’ is a corruption or variation of Dāityā, then the region west of Ural river may be rightly be identified as Airyana Vaēǰah, the homeland of Indo-Iranians.

But does this region have the river Raŋhā to its west? Well, it turns out that the Scythians called the Volga river ‘Rha’ which is cognate to Raŋhā. Both Ural and Volga drain into the Caspian sea, which may be identified with the Vourukaša. Vouru is an adjective meaning “wide”, and the name “Kasha” could have easily become the basis for the name “Caspian”.

Therefore, the Airyana Vaēǰah may be identified as the land north of Caspian sea, between Ural and Volga rivers. Today, this region corresponds to northwestern Kazakhstan. Agreeing with the Avestan description, the region is extremely cold, and temperature only rises above 20 degree centigrade during the months June-August, and peaks at 25 degree centigrade.

At the Sri Vidyashankara Temple (constructed in 1338) at Sringeri, among thedvarapalakas(door guardians) situated outside the sanctum of the presiding deity, there is a sculpture of Nandi with a monkey-like face.

One may read about this temple here:

[https://www.sringeri.net/temples/sri-vidyashankara](https://www.sringeri.net/temples/sri-vidyashankara)

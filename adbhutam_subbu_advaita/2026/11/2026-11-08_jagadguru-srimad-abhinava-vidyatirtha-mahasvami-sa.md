Jagadguru-Srimad-Abhinava-Vidyatirtha-Mahasvami-sadhana-jivanmuktyapti-lila-shatakam (Kannada)

A hundred verses portray the matchless sadhana and Self-realisation of HH Jagadguru Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswamin, the 35th Shankaracharya of Sringeri Sharada Peetham. He attained Brahma-sakshatkara at 18 and remained established in the Supreme Brahman as a Jivanmukta. Based on accounts personally imparted by Him, the verses, with English translation and insightful notes, recount His sadhana and attainment of Jivanmukti, highlighting the momentous final 7 days. The present Jagadguru, HH Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahasannidhanam, reviewed, refined and blessed their publication.

This Kannada version is published on Nov. 8, 2026, the 110th sacred Jayanthi of HH Jagadguru Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswamin by Centre for Brahmavidya, with salutations at His lotus feet.

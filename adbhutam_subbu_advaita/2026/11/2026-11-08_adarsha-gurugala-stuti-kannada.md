Adarsha Gurugala Stuti (Kannada)

HH Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswamin, the 35th Jagadguru Shankaracharya of Sringeri Sharada Peetham, is extolled as the Supreme Brahman, Ishvara and a consummate Jivanmukta in two profound and inspiring hymns spontaneously composed by the present Jagadguru Shankaracharya, HH Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahasannidhanam. In Praise of the Perfect Guru comprises these hymns with English translations and illuminating commentaries that unfold their magnificent spiritual vision. HH Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahasannidhanam, the present Jagadguru, graciously reviewed the work, approved it and blessed its publication.

This Kannada version is published on Nov. 8, 2026, the 110th sacred Jayanthi of HH Jagadguru Sri Abhinava Vidyatirtha Mahaswamin by Centre for Brahmavidya, with reverential salutations at His lotus feet.

Sri G.B.Kulkarni, the compiler-author, writes:  
  
GARLAND OF STORIES

Friends,

The Journey of Garland of Stories started on 29, June 2021 coming to an end Today 25th June 2026. During these five years I could share 300 stories, comprising of General stories 150, Vedantic stories 65, Devotional stories 15, Zen Stories 40, Sufi Stories 20, and Judaism Stories 10. In this file you will find Compiler’s Preface, Readers’ Reviews and 300 stories. Each story is of just one page only and it contains, Title, Image, the story and Moral – all in one page. My special thanks to all the Reviews from friends and others who have encouraged me to do this. Happy to share that during these five years there is no break, every Thursday a story was shared. This was all possible because of God’s Grace and your support and encouragements.

May I humbly request all of you to open the above file and share your views. Kindly share this file to all your other groups.

  
Please download the Book by clicking here: <https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/garland-of-300-stories-compressed.pdf>

With Warm Regards  
G B Kulkarni  
25th June 2026.

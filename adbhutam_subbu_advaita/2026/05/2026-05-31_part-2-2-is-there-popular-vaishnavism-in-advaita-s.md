There has been a misconception that Sri Adi Shankaracharya was a Vaishnava, and that this can be inferred from his Bhāṣya corpus. This is 2-part video (of which part 1 is uploaded here
[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQi3LkrZ74E](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQi3LkrZ74E)• Is there popular Vaishnavism in Shankara Advaita… ) sets at rest such a thinking by providing appropriate evidence from the very Bhāṣya sources.

Part 1 features Insights from Brahmasri Dr. Jammalamadaka Srinivas ji

This video, Part 2/2 features highlights from Vidwan Marella Kirthi Chandra.

The following two articles capture the points presented in this video:

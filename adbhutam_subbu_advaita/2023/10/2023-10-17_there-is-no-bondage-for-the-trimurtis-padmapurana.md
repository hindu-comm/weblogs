There is no bondage for the Trimurtis – Padmapurana

In this chapter Paramashiva says:

पद्मपुराणम्/खण्डः १ (सृष्टिखण्डम्)/अध्यायः ७८

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/y6c](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/y6c)

शिव उवाच-

लोकानां च हितार्थाय पृथग्भूता युगे युगे।

सर्वं कुर्मो वयं पुत्र ब्रह्मविष्णुमहेश्वराः२०।

नास्माकं बंधमोक्षौ च नाकार्यं कार्यमेव वा।

तथा लोकस्य रक्षार्थं चरामो विधिपूर्वकम्२१।

In every era we the Trimurtis do all the work separately for the welfare of the world. There is neither bondage nor liberation for us. And there is no enjoined or prohibited karma for us.

From this statement it is clear that there is no real difference in the Trimurtis. And it is also known that none of the three is a jiva.

Om

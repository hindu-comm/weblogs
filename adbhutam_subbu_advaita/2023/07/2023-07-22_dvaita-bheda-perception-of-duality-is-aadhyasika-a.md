Narada’s sermon on Varnashrama Dharma to Yudhishthira has this message:

श्रीमद्भागवतपुराणम्/स्कन्धः ७/अध्यायः १२

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/zh1](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/zh1)

कल्पयित्वाऽऽत्मना यावद् आभासमिदमीश्वरः ।

द्वैतं तावन्न विरमेत् ततो ह्यस्य विपर्ययः ॥ १० ॥

As long as one does not give up distinctions like ‘this is a man, this is a woman’, then the superimposition of qualities in those bereft of qualities, will continue. That is, there is a misunderstanding, viparaya, perceiving one as the other. One has to let it go through acquiring right knowledge. Otherwise, bondage will be a never-ending story.

This indicates that the idea of gender, male and female, is not present in the true nature of the Atman. It is certainly a superimposition, a vision of error, in the Atman.

Sridhara Swami’s commentary is given here: [https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/cgXQK_2brAo](https://groups.google.com/g/advaitin/c/cgXQK_2brAo)

Om

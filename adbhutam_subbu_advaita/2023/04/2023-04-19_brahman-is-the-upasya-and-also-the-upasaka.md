Brahman is the Upasya and also the Upasaka

In the Ahalya prayer to Rama in the below link:

अहल्याकृतरामस्तोत्रम्  
[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/7sr](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/7sr)

From the Adhyatma Ramayanam, there is a very exalted concept:

अहल्योवाचः ।

अहो कृतार्थाऽस्मि जगन्निवास ते पादाब्जसंल्लग्नरजःकणादहम् ।  
स्पृशामि यत्पद्मजशङ्करादिभिर्विमृग्यते रन्धितमानसैः सदा ॥ १॥

अयं हि विश्वोद्भवसंयमानामेकः स्वमायागुणबिम्बितो यः ।  
विरिञ्चिविष्ण्वीश्वरनामभेदान् धत्ते स्वतन्त्रः परिपूर्ण आत्मा ॥ ८॥

Look at the two verses here. The first one says The dust of Your feet is searched for by Brahma and Shiva, etc. with great fervor. The second one says: You have taken the forms of Brahma and Shiva and Vishnu.

This is the beauty of Veda Vyasa’s method: The upasya is no different from the upasaka. The seeker and the sought are one.

Om

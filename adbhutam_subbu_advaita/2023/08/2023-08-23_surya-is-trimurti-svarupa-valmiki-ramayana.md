In the Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda, is the famous Aditya Hridayam stotram that Agastya Muni imparts to Sri Rama. One can read the same here with English translation:

[http://www.valmikiramayan.net/yuddha/sarga105/yuddha_105_frame.htm](http://www.valmikiramayan.net/yuddha/sarga105/yuddha_105_frame.htm)

Therein it is stated:

brahmeshaanaachyuteshaaya suuryaayaadityavarchase \|  
bhaasvate sarvabhakShaaya raudraaya vapuShe namaH \|\| 6-105-19

“Hail to you, the ruler of Brahma, Lord Shiva and Lord Vishnu the infallible, the sun-god, the light indwelling the solar orb, the resplendent one, the devourer of all, appearing in the form of Rudra.”

That Surya is ‘sarva devatA svarupa’ is also stated there:

eSha brahmaa cha viShNushcha shivaH skandaH prajaapatiH \|  
mahendro dhanadaH kaalo yamaH somo hyapaaMpatiH \|\| 6-105-8

“He is Brahma the creator, Vishnu the protector of the Universe, Lord Shiva the god of destruction, Skanda (son of Lord Shiva), Prajapati (the ten lords of creted beings), Indra the ruler of gods, Kubera the bestower of riches, Kala the Time-spirit, Yama the god of retribution, Soma the moon-god and Varuna the ruler of the waters.”

Sri Appayya Dikshitar in his ‘Sri Ramayana Tatparya sara sangraha stotram’ cites the first verse above and says that Surya’s antaryami is Rudra based on this line:

sarvabhakShaaya raudraaya vapuShe namaH \|\| 6-105-19 //sarva bhakShaaya= the devourer of all; raudraaya vapuShe= appearing in the form of Rudra.//

He cites some mantra passages of the Krishna Yajur Veda ‘Sri Rudram’ which he says are ‘Aditya Upasana mantras’.

The name ‘hiranya retaah’ as one in the 108 names of Shiva and also in the Shiva Sahasranama, is present in the Aditya Hridayam too: SuvarNasadR^isho bhaanur hiraNyaretaa divaakaraH \|\| 6-105-10

Om Tat Sat

Adbhutam's Blog

In this chapter 86 of the Linga Purana, containing about 157 verses, there is a very detailed Adhyatma Advaita Sadhana.

लिङ्गपुराणम् – पूर्वभागः/अध्यायः ८६  
[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/4iy](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/4iy)

A lot of teaching on Vairagya, dhyana, Jnana nishtha, nature of sense organs, etc. can be seen here.

By reading the verses with or without English translation, one can quickly recognize innumerable Upanishadic passages annotated there: Mandukya, Mundaka, KaTha, Kaivalya, Narayana Suktam, Taittiriya, etc.

[https://archive.org/details/LingaPuranaJ.L.ShastriPart1/page/n445/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/LingaPuranaJ.L.ShastriPart1/page/n445/mode/2up)

The Advaitic purport is so explicit that one cannot miss appreciating it all through.

The Pashupata Yoga itself, as a means to Moksha, is well taught in the Atharva Shiraa Upanishad.

[https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_upanishhat/atharvashira.html?lang=sa](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_upanishhat/atharvashira.html?lang=sa)

What is striking is that this ‘Tamasa’ purana, which is claimed to lead one to hell, naraka, is not teaching adharma at all. Only if it teaches adharma can one say it will lead to hell. But a study of this chapter, as a sample, will reveal that the claim that it leads to naraka, hell, is falsified.

The Atharva shira Upanishad in Linga Purana:

[https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/archives/advaita-l/2020-January/053796.html](https://www.advaita-vedanta.org/archives/advaita-l/2020-January/053796.html)

Om Tat Sat

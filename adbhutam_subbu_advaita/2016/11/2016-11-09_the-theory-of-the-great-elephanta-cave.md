An interesting article published in the July 1852 issue of the JOURNAL OF THE BOMBAY BRANCH OF THE R0YAL ASIATIC S0CIETY.

[http://www.aiktcdspace.org:8080/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1214/16/JASB-VOLUME%204%20FOR%20July%2c%201852\_\_ISSUE%20NO%2016.pdf  
](http://www.aiktcdspace.org:8080/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1214/16/JASB-VOLUME%204%20FOR%20July%2c%201852__ISSUE%20NO%2016.pdf)

One has to go to page 108 of the pdf (261 on the book) where the article titled

‘The Theory of the Great Elephanta Cave’ By the. Reverend J. STEVENSON, D.D. Presented 13th May 1852.’ is found. The entire article spans the pages 108 to 122 of the pdf (261 to 275 of the book).

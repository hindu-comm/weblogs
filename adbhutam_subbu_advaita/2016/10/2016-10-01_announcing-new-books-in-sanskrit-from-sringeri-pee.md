Announcing New Books in Sanskrit from Sringeri Peetham

Here are the links to know more about the new books:

[https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/YZkWQFisX90](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/YZkWQFisX90)

[https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/BY1C4dCW9T4](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/BY1C4dCW9T4)

[https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/5a47_cRzXAM](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/bvparishat/5a47_cRzXAM)

Sri Madhusudana Saraswati’s commentary on the Bhagavadgītā, ‘Gūḍhārtha Dīpikā’, has been translated to Kannada and published by Vedanta Bharati recently. Prof.M.A.Hegde, Siddapura, has done the translation. The book, in A4 size, Hardbound, in over 600 pages, is priced at Rs.450. Available with:

Sri Nagaraj, Ramashankaraprasada, 22nd Main,BSK 2nd stage (opp. Aurobindo school) Phone: 9449912121

Sringeri Shankara Maṭha, Book Stall, Bangalore 560004.

A complete translation of theMādhavīya Shankara Vijaya in Kannada along with footnotes by MM Vidwan (late) Sri Ranganatha Sharma is now published and available in two volumes.

The subsidised value is Rs.150 each. Pl. contact:

Sri K.Srinivasan, Vidyabhārati Press, Shankarapuram, Bangalore. Phone: 94488 10668  email:srinivasan.rbi@[gmail.com](http://gmail.com/)

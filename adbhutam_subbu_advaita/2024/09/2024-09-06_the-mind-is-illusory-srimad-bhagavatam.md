In the Bhagavatam there is the famous discourse of the Supreme Truth between the Jnani, Bharata, and King Rahugana. In this discourse the Jnani tells the King how the mind plays tricks and that one has to be extremely careful about it. Devotion to the Guru and Bhagavan is the weapon that tackles the mind.

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/pd0](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/pd0)

भ्रातृव्यमेनं तददभ्रवीर्यमुपेक्षयाध्येधितमप्रमत्तः  
गुरोर्हरेश्चरणोपासनास्त्रो जहि**व्यलीकं**स्वयमात्ममोषम् १७

[https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/5/11/17/](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/5/11/17/)

[*bhrātṛvyam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=bhr%C4%81t%E1%B9%9Bvyam)— the formidable enemy;[*enam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=enam)— this mind;[*tat*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=tat)— that;[*adabhra*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=adabhra)–[*vīryam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=v%C4%ABryam)— very, very powerful;[*upekṣayā*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=upek%E1%B9%A3ay%C4%81)— by neglecting;[*adhyedhitam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=adhyedhitam)— unnecessarily increased in power;[*apramattaḥ*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=apramatta%E1%B8%A5)— one who is without illusion;[*guroḥ*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=guro%E1%B8%A5)— of the spiritual master;[*hareḥ*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=hare%E1%B8%A5)— of the Supreme Personality of Godhead;[*caraṇa*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=cara%E1%B9%87a)— of the lotus feet;[*upāsanā*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=up%C4%81san%C4%81)–[*astraḥ*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=astra%E1%B8%A5)— applying the weapon of worshiping;[*jahi*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=jahi)— conquer;**[*vyalīkam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=vyal%C4%ABkam)— false;**[*svayam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=svayam)— personally;[*ātma*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=%C4%81tma)–[*moṣam*](https://vedabase.io/en/search/synonyms/?original=mo%E1%B9%A3am)— which covers the constitutional position of the living entity.

## Translation

This uncontrolled mind is the greatest enemy of the living entity. If one neglects it or gives it a chance, it will grow more and more powerful and will become victorious. Although it is not factual, it is very strong. It covers the constitutional position of the soul. O King, please try to conquer this mind by the weapon of service to the lotus feet of the spiritual master and of the Supreme Personality of Godhead. Do this with great care.

Sridhara Swamin gives the meaning for the word: vyalIkam:
mithyAbhUtam. 

Om Tat Sat

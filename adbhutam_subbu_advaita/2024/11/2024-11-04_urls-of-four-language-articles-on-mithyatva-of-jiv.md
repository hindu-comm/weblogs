URLs of Four Language Articles on ‘Mithyatva of Jiva-Jagat in Bhagavatam’

श्रीमद्भागवते चिदचितोः (चेतनाचेतनयोः = जीवजगतोः) मिथ्यात्वनिरूपणम्

The Bhagavatam is replete with statements for:

1.  Brahman itself appearing as the Jiva (Chit) and the Jagat (Achit) 2.  That the jiva is unreal and so is the Jagat – Chit and Achit are
    both unreal, leaving Brahman alone to be Real.

Here are articles on the above theme in Four Languages. One can choose to read the article in the language of his choice.

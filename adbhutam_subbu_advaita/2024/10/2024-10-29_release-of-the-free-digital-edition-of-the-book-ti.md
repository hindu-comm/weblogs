Release of the free digital edition of the book ‘Timeless Teachings’ by the lotus hands of His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Vidhuśekhara-bhāratī Svāmin

This eBook, published by the Centre for Brahmavidya, contains the translation of the sacred Vedantic discourses based on the Brahma Sūtrasdelivered by His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin,the revered 36thJagadguru Shankaracharya of the Dakshinamnaya Sringeri Sharada Peethamin 2012 at Chennai.

The ebook was released by His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Vidhuśekhara-bhāratī Svāmin on the occasion of the Suvarna Bharathi Mahotsavam at Chennai on 29th October 2024, (āśvina-kṛṣṇa-dvādaśī), the 50th anniversary of His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin’s taking up of saṁnyāsa in 1974.

Link for the free download: [https://centreforbrahmavidya.org/files/download/Timeless-Teachings.pdf](https://centreforbrahmavidya.org/files/download/Timeless-Teachings.pdf)

Video/Audio recordings ofHis Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin’s Vedanta classes:-

The Centre for Brahmavidya is privileged to make available online the first batch of the video recordings of His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin’s classes on Śrī Śaṅkara-bhagavatpāda’s Brahma-sūtra-bhāṣya with the gloss, Ratnaprabhā, and all the audio recordings of His Holiness’s classes on the Taittirīya-upaniṣad-bhāṣya with the gloss, Vanamālā.

The recordings were released by His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Vidhuśekhara-bhāratī Svāmin on the occasion of the Suvarna Bharathi Mahotsavam at Chennai on 29th October 2024, (āśvina-kṛṣṇa-dvādaśī), the 50th anniversary of His Holiness Jagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin’s taking up of saṁnyāsa in 1974.

The recordings of these classes provide a unique opportunity for all to hear, imbibe, delight in, and greatly benefit from the sublime exposition of Vedānta by His HolinessJagadguru Śrī Bharatī-tīrtha Mahāsvāmin, an undisputed authority on Nyāya and Vedānta and the revered 36th Jagadguru Shankaracharya of the Dakshinamnaya Sringeri Sharada Peetham.  
The classes can be viewed on the website of the Centre for Brahmavidya:

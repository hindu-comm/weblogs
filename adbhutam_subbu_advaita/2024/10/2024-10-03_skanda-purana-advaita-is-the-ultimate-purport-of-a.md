स्मृतीनां च पुराणानां भारतस्य तथैव च ।  
वेदानुसारिविद्यानामन्यासामास्तिकोत्तमाः ॥ ६८॥

शैवागमानां सर्वेषां विष्णुप्रोक्तागमस्य च ।  
अस्मदुक्तागमस्यापि सुराः सूक्ष्मनिरूपणे ॥ ६९॥

बुद्धागमानां सर्वेषां तथैवार्हागमस्य च ।  
यक्षगन्धर्वसिद्धादिनिर्मितस्यागमस्य च ॥ ७०॥

परमाद्वैतविज्ञानं कस्य मर्त्यस्य सिध्यति ।  
कस्य देवस्य वा साक्षाच्छिवस्यैव हि सिध्यति ॥ ७१॥

~ श्रीस्कन्दपुराणे सूतसंहितायां ब्रह्मगीतासूपनिषत्सु तलवकारोपनिषद्व्याख्याकथनं चतुर्थोऽध्यायः ॥ ४॥

Verse 68: Of the Smritis, Puranas, and the Bharata (Mahabharata), And of the sciences that follow the Vedas, and others – O best of the faithful!

Verse 69: Of all the Shaiva Agamas, and the Agamas spoken by Vishnu, And even of the Agamas spoken by us, O gods, in subtle examination,

Verse 70: Of all the Buddhist Agamas, and likewise of the Jain Agamas, And of the Agamas created by Yakshas, Gandharvas, Siddhas, and others,

Verse 71: The knowledge of Supreme Non-dualism, for which mortal does it become perfected? For which god does it become directly perfected? Indeed, only for Shiva. The idea is: There is no mortal in reality; it is Shiva, Brahman, alone that appears as the bound jiva and the released one.

Om Tat Sat

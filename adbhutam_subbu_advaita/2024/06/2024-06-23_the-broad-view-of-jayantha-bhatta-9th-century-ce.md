There is no basis, justification, for Shaiva-Vaishnava quarrels – says Jayantha Bhatta, a vaidika, of 9th Century CE in his Nyayamanjari.

[https://jainqq.org/booktext/Nyayamanjari_Part_01/002265](https://jainqq.org/booktext/Nyayamanjari_Part_01/002265)

The ‘nyayamanjari’ is a renowned work of Jayantha Bhatta[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayanta_Bhatta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayanta_Bhatta?fbclid=IwAR0UphdOntNmD6njSRwjOec3vYy_22jN-hfkMPCGSNArUSuhh8uSzIA0xKg)This work is a detailed exposition of the Nyaya sutras of Gautama. Under the section of Veda praamaaNya, Jayantha Bhatta discusses the issues related to the Veda vs. Agama problem. The entire discussion, in simple Sanskrit, is available in the link shown above. Just one aspect is highlighted here: \[there are several typos in the above page. Incidentally, Jayantha Bhatta, while refuting the Vedanta Darshana, along with Sankhya, etc. has considered Shankara’s Advaita as the purva paksha and made a detailed refutation. One can see the analogies, arguments, etc. present in Shaankara Bhashya replicated while refuting.\]

In order to see the embedded comments please read from the linked page.

**शैववैष्णवकलहस्य निर्मूलत्वम्**

\[The baselessness of the Shaiva-Vaishnava tussle\]

एकस्य कस्यचिदशेषजगत्प्रसूति-

हेतोरनादिपुरुषस्य महाविभूतेः ।

सृष्टिस्थितिप्रलयकार्यविभागयोगात्

ब्रह्मेति विष्णुरिति रुद्र इति प्रतीतिः ॥ ८३ ॥

\[In this verse, Jayantha Bhatta says ‘One Beginningless, Infinite, Purusha, of great splendour, who is the One complete cause of the creation, sustenance and destruction, owing to these very cosmic functions, comes to be called Brahma, Vishnu and Rudra.\]

The embedded commentary in the above citation cites the famous Vishnu Purana verse:

**सृष्टिस्थित्यन्तकरणीं ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवात्मिकाम् ।  
स संज्ञां याति भगवान् एक एव जनार्दनःवि. पु. १-२-६६**

The meaning of the above verse is the same as what is given for Jayantha Bhatta’s verse.

**This verse is quoted by Shankara in Vishnu Sahasranama Bhasya for
Abheda.**

इत्याद्यप्यत्र द्रष्टव्यम् ॥

शैवपञ्चरात्रोक्तधर्माणां वैदिकत्वम्

**\[The Vedic nature of the practices taught in the Shaiva and Pancharatra Agamas\]**

वेदे च पदे पदे**एक एव रुद्रोऽवतस्थे न द्वितीयः अथ. ३,  
इदं विष्णुर्विचक्रमे**तै. सं. १-२-१३, ऋ. सं. १-२२-७ इति रुद्रो  
विष्णुश्च पठ्यते । तद्योगाश्च तदाराधनोपाया वेदेऽपि चोदिता  
एव । शैवपञ्चरात्रयोस्तु तद्योगा एवा216न्यथोपदिश्यन्ते । न चैष  
I एव वेदविरोधः; वैकल्पिकत्वादुपायानाम् । अतः आप्तप्रणीतत्वात्,  
वेदाविरुद्धत्वाच्च न तयोरप्रामाण्यम् ॥

\[Jayantha Bhatta says: In the Veda, repeatedly we hear**‘One Rudra alone, none second to him**(Atharvashikha/shira, Shwetashvatara Upanishad). And**‘idam vishnurvichakrame’**Tai Samhita 1.2.13, Rg.samhita1.22.7 thus about Rudra and Vishnu. The method of worshiping these deities is also taught in the Veda itself. In the Shaiva and Pancharatra Agma-s these methods alone have been taught in a different way. This much is no ground to hold the latter to be contradicting the Veda as methods are always optional. Therefore, since these Agamas have been composed by ‘Apta-s’, venerable ones, and also since these do not contradict the Veda, the two stated Agama-s are quite valid.\]

Shankara says in the Pancharatradhikaran of the Brahmasutra:

यदपि तस्य भगवतोऽभिगमनादिलक्षणमाराधनमजस्रमनन्यचित्ततयाभिप्रेयते, तदपि न प्रतिषिध्यते, श्रुतिस्मृत्योरीश्वरप्रणिधानस्य प्रसिद्धत्वात् ।

\[Worshiping the Supreme Lord in various ways is well-known in the Sruti Smruti.\]

This highly catholic view of Jayantha Bhatta, of the 9th Century, is very commendable. His another work ‘AagamaDambara’, a drama, is also full of such concepts.

In conclusion, I am copying two images from the Editor Sri V.Raghavan’s note to the AagamaDambara that captures Jayantha Bhatta’s thought and also the attitude that prevailed in those distant times.

Āgamaḍambaraof Jayanta Bhaṭṭa

Dr.V.Raghavan:

[https://archive.org/details/AgamaDambara/page/n25/mode/2up](https://archive.org/details/AgamaDambara/page/n25/mode/2up)

नानाविधैरागममार्गभेदेरादिश्यमाना बहवोऽभ्युपायाः ।  
एकत्र ते श्रेयसि संपतन्ति सिन्धी प्रवाहा इव जाह्नवीयाः \|\|

प्रवेष्टुकामा बहवः पुमांसः पुरे यथैकत्र महागृहे वा ।

द्वारान्तरेणापि विशन्ति केचित् तथोत्तमे धाम्नि मुमुक्षवोऽपि ॥

(Nyayamanjari of Jayanta Bhatta)

1\. Raghuvamsha X.26  
  
बहुधाप्यागमैर्भिन्नाः पन्थानः सिद्धिहेतवः ।

त्वय्येव निपतन्त्योघा जाह्नवीया इवार्णवे ॥

2.**Pushpadanta’s Mahimnastava: 7**

त्रयी सांख्यं योगः पशुपतिमतं वैष्णवमिति

प्रमिन्ने प्रस्थाने परमिदमदः पथ्यमिति च ।

रुचीनां वैचित्र्यादृजुकुटिलनानापथजुषां

नृणामेको गम्यस्त्वमसि पयसामर्णव इव ॥

परमं पुरुषार्थं प्रति न चागमानां विरोधिता काचित् ।

आदिश्यते हि सर्वैः कैवल्यं तुल्यमेव फलम् ॥

एकोऽप्यसौ सकलसत्त्वहिताय काय-  
मिच्छाविनिर्मितमनेकमुपादधानः ।  
नानागमानुपदिशन् विविधा विभर्ति

तास्ताः समस्तभुवनप्रथिताः समाख्याः ॥

एकः शिवः पशुपतिः कपिलोऽथ विष्णुः  
संकर्षणो जिनमुनिः सुगतो मनुर्वा  
संज्ञाः परं पृथगिमास्तनवोऽपि काम-  
मव्याकृते तु परमात्मनि नास्ति भेदः ॥

All the above, summarizing the sayings of many famous people, say in purport:

There are many different ways to attain the same spiritual goal.

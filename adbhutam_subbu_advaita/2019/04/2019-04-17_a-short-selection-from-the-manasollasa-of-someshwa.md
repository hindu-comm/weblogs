A short selection from the Manasollasa of Someshwara III

<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manasollasa>

//The***Mānasollāsa***, also known as***Abhilashitartha Chintamani***, is an early 12th-century Sanskrit text composed by the[Kalyani Chalukya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalyani_Chalukya_dynasty)king[Someshvara III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Someshvara_III), who ruled in present-day[South India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India). It is an encyclopedic work covering topics such as polity, governance, ethics, economics, astronomy, astrology, rhetoric, veterinary medicine, horticulture, perfumes, food, architecture, sports, painting, poetry and music. The text is a valuable source of socio-cultural information on 11th- and 12th-century India.//

Excerpts from this book covering the deities worshiped at that time are collected and presented here:

## Online Bhagavad Gita Course Announcement

The Bhagavad Gita has inspired humanity for millennia because it addresses life’s  
most fundamental questions. The Gita is also an apt text for current times because it elaborates on ways to equip oneself emotionally and spiritually. Let us take the place of Arjuna, learn what Krishna has to say, and enrich our life.

## Highlights

• Verse by verse Explanation  
• Weekly online video class  
• Satsang (Q&A)  
• Timing to suit Indian & US residents

Teacher:  
Dr. Jayakumar S. Ammangudi \[B.Tech(IIT). MS. PhD.\]  
Studied Vedanta, Sanskrit for 20+ years under Swami Dayananda Saraswati ji of Arsha Vidya Gurukulam.

Serious students may please follow the link below to know more and register for this long term course:  
[https://tinyurl.com/ASK-BG-Reg](https://tinyurl.com/ASK-BG-Reg)

Please share this in other groups.

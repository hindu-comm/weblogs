A program for the devout pārāyaṇam of the 108 names addressed to Sri Shankaracharya is initiated by the Jagadguru HH Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswaminaḥ. HH Sri Sannidhanam, Jagadguru Sri Sri Vidhushekhara Bharati Mahaswaminaḥ has given a brief benedictory address in Kannada on the greatness and need for the parayanam. The other details of the program are available here:

[http://www.sringeri.net/ashtottara-yajna](http://www.sringeri.net/ashtottara-yajna)

All Advaitins are welcome to involve themselves in this program in whatever possible manner. Please also forward this message to all your friends and other groups. The guidelines for the conduct of the program is available.below.

warm regards

subrahmanian.v

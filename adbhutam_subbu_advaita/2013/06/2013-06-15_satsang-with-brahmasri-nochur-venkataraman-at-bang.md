Announcing a satsang with Brahmasri Nochur Venkataraman at Bangalore on Saturday the **22nd June, 2013** at BeNNe Govindappa Kalyana mantapa, Karanji Circle, Gandhi Bazaar, Bangalore 560004 from **5.00 to 6.30 PM**. **The talk by the revered speaker will be in English**. Contact organizers at: Phone 080-26714992 and Sri Gopalakrishna 9845121153.

Here is a brief profile of Br.Sri Nochur Venkataraman:

[http://legendsinmusic.ucoz. com/index/sri_nochur\_ venkataraman/0-20](http://legendsinmusic.ucoz.com/index/sri_nochur_venkataraman/0-20)

Sri Nochur Venkatraman belongs to the parampara of sages like Adi Sankara and Ramana Maharishi. He is not a ‘swami’ in the usual sense and yet devotees call him so considering his realization.Upanishadic wisdom flows through him with unsurpassed clarity. His discourses on bhakti and jnana reflect the genuine source of wisdom in him. A brilliant speaker with great knowledge of Sanskrit, Sri Nochur Venkataraman hails from Nochur village near Palakad in Kerala. Proficient in Malayalam and Tamil, Nochur’s upanyasam of Srimad Bhagavatham is a treat to listen.

Pl. inform your friends about the satsang and the change in venue.

He is one YouTube too.

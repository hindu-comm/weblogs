A mahAtmA by name Mysore Sri Shivarama Sastry lived over a hundred years ago in Mysore. He was initially employed in the Mysore Palace. Owing to strong dispassion he renounced householder’s life and became a recluse.
He composed hundreds of songs in Kannada on the Vedantic theme. His compositions are extempore and bring out the profound tattva in instantly appealing poetry. Swami Paramananda Bharati took steps to publish about 150 of these songs and has also got them recorded
rendered by expert musicians.

Here are two links on google drive for a few of the songs rendered to music:

[https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QMcfQjP7eGwbVgw_t2Vo772hAzIUXb_I?usp=sharing](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1QMcfQjP7eGwbVgw_t2Vo772hAzIUXb_I?usp=sharing)

I have deep appreciation for the compositions which Kannada-knowing people will surely like to benefit from.

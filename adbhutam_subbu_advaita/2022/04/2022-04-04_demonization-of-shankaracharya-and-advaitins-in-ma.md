Adbhutam's Blog

It is not widely known to both Advaitins, even scholars, and laymen and the general public that the Madhva literature, starting from Madhwacharya himself, till today, has a lot of material that demonizes Shankaracharya and Advaitins. This is in Sanskrit, Kannada and English. The following URL has a file, which is only representative and not exhaustive, of such instances. To the present day Madhwa scholars discourse on this in public domain and Madhwa Mutts encourage and reward such scholars for doing this.

[Click to access instance-samples-of-shankaracharya-and-advaiti-nindaa-in-madhwa-books.pdf](https://adbhutam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/instance-samples-of-shankaracharya-and-advaiti-nindaa-in-madhwa-books.pdf)

Om.

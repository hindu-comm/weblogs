# हरिवंशपुराणम्/पर्व २ (विष्णुपर्व)/अध्यायः १२५

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1coh](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1coh)

[https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1coh](https://sa.wikisource.org/s/1coh)

This chapter consists anenchanting Stuti of the identity of Hari Hara and Brahma by Markandeya.There are many interesting aspects in this Stuti.And English translation can be found here:

[Click to access A_Prose_English_Translation_of_Harivamsh.pdf](https://ia601007.us.archive.org/24/items/AProseEnglishTranslationOfHarivamsh/A_Prose_English_Translation_of_Harivamsh.pdf)

on pages 790 last paragraph onwards.

To read the stuti in its original gives great joy.

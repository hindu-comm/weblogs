Who is the entity referred in BG 18.62 as ‘Him = tam’?

In the Bh.Gita we have this verse:  
तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत।  
तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्तिं स्थानं प्राप्स्यसि शाश्वतम्।।18.62।।  
8.62 Take refuge in Him alone with your whole being, O scion of the Bharata dynasty. Through His grace you will attain the supreme Peace and the eternal Abode.

A question arises as to what or who is meant by the pronoun ‘Him’ in this verse. In order to know this, we have to know who/what is the noun that is at the basis of this pronoun. The earlier verse there has this:  
ईश्वरः सर्वभूतानां हृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति।  
भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया।।18.61।।

18.61 O Arjuna, the Lord resides in the region of the heart of all creatures, revolving through Maya all the creatures (as though) mounted on a machine!  
Shankaracharya’s commentary here is:  
ईश्वरःईशनशीलःनारायणःसर्वभूतानांसर्वप्राणिनांहृद्देशेहृदयदेशेअर्जुन..तिष्ठतिस्थितिंलभते।तेषुसःकथंतिष्ठतीति,आह—भ्रामयन्भ्रमणंकारयन्सर्वभूतानियन्त्रारूढानियन्त्राणिआरूढानिअधिष्ठितानिइव—इतिइवशब्दःअत्रद्रष्टव्यः—यथादारुकृतपुरुषादीनियन्त्रारूढानि।माययाच्छद्मनाभ्रामयन्तिष्ठतिइतिसम्बन्धः॥ ६१ ॥

18.61 Arjuna, Isvarah, the Lord , Narayana the Ruler; tisthati, resides, remains seated; hrd-dese, in the region of the heart; sarva-bhutanam, of all creatures, of all living beings. How does He reside? In answer the Lord says: bhramayan, revolving; mayaya, through Maya, through delusion; sarva-bhutani, all the creatures; as though yantra-arudhani, mounted on a machine-like man’ etc., made of wood, mounted on a machine. The word iva (as though) has to be thus understood here. Bhramayan, revolving, is to be connected with tisthati, resides (conveying the idea, ‘resides৷৷.while revolving’).

In order to know what Shankara means by the term Narayana here, we can see where else Shankara has cited this verse and what meaning he intends there.

Brahma Sutra Bbhashya 1.2.6:  
स्मृतिश्च शारीरपरमात्मनोर्भेदं दर्शयति —[‘ईश्वरःसर्वभूतानांहृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति। भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया’ (भ. गी. १८। ६१)](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=18&id=BG_C18_V61&hl=%E0%A4%88%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A0%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%C2%A0%E0%A5%A4%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE)इत्याद्या।The smriti too talks of difference between the individual (jiva) and the Supreme (Paramatma) in the Bh.18.61. \[The ‘difference’ is: the jiva is said to be impelled by Ishwara, who is someone else, other than the jiva.\] The siddhantin in reply says:
अत्राह—कःपुनरयंशारीरोनामपरमात्मनोऽन्यः,यःप्रतिषिध्यते— ‘अनुपपत्तेस्तुनशारीरः’इत्यादिना?श्रुतिस्तु[‘नान्योऽतोऽस्तिद्रष्टानान्योऽतोऽस्तिश्रोता’ (बृ. उ. ३। ७। २३)](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=3&id=BR_C03_S07_V23&hl=%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A4%BE%20%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AF%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE)इत्येवंजातीयकापरमात्मनोऽन्यमात्मानंवारयति।तथास्मृतिरपि[‘क्षेत्रज्ञंचापिमांविद्धिसर्वक्षेत्रेषुभारत’ (भ. गी. १३। २)](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=13&id=BG_C13_V02&hl=%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%9A%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%81%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%A4)इत्येवंजातीयकेति।\[Who is that individual jiva who is different from the Supreme..? The Upanishads such as Br.Up.3.7.23 says: There is none other (other than the Supreme) that is the seer, hearer, etc. negate the idea that the Supreme is someone different than the individual. The Bh.Gita 13.2 too has this teaching alone: Know Me to the be knower (individual) of the field.\]  
अत्रोच्यते—सत्यमेवैतत्—परएवात्मादेहेन्द्रियमनोबुद्ध्युपाधिभिःपरिच्छिद्यमानोबालैःशारीरइत्युपचर्यते;यथाघटकरकाद्युपाधिवशादपरिच्छिन्नमपिनभःपरिच्छिन्नवदवभासते,तद्वत्।तदपेक्षयाचकर्मकर्तृत्वादिभेदव्यवहारोनविरुध्यतेप्राक्‘तत्त्वमसि’इत्यात्मैकत्वोपदेशग्रहणात्।गृहीतेत्वात्मैकत्वेबन्धमोक्षादिसर्वव्यवहारपरिसमाप्तिरेवस्यात्॥ ६ ॥  
\[True indeed. The Supreme Self alone through the upadhis, adjiuncts, that are the body, organs, mind, etc, restricted, is spoken of as the individual jiva by those who lack discriminating intellect, baala. Just as the unlimited ether appears as though limited due to the enclosures set up by pot, jar, etc.adjuncts. Based on this (wrong understanding), the doership, etc. for the Self is not contradictory, prior to the grasping of the teaching of non-difference by the passages such as ‘Tat tvam asi’, You are That. When this teaching of Oneness of the Self is understood, all transactions involving bondage and liberation come to an end indeed. \]  
So, here is an instance of Shankara citing the BG 18.61 where the ‘Ishwara’ commented upon by Shankara as ‘Narayana’ is taught as none other than the Supreme-consciousness.that is non-different from the jiva-consciousness. The finite jiva can never be identified with Narayana. Hence the ‘Narayana’ that Shankara refers to cannot be a deity by that popular name.     
BSB 2.1.14: \[Here the verse ‘[ईश्वरःसर्वभूतानांहृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=18&id=BG_C18_V61&hl=%20%E0%A4%88%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A0%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%C2%A0%E0%A5%A4%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE)‘ as applying to vyavaharika too, i.e. bheda is admitted in vyavahara.:  
इति परमार्थावस्थायामीशित्रीशितव्यादिव्यवहाराभावः प्रदर्श्यते ; व्यवहारावस्थायां तूक्तः श्रुतावपीश्वरादिव्यवहारः —[‘ एष सर्वेश्वर एष भूताधिपतिरेष भूतपाल एष सेतुर्विधरण एषां लोकानामसम्भेदाय’ (बृ. उ. ४। ४। २२)](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Brha?page=4&id=BR_C04_S04_V22&hl=%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B7%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B7%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B7%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B2%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B7%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%A3%20%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B2%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%A6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF)इति ; तथा चेश्वरगीतास्वपि —[‘ ईश्वरःसर्वभूतानांहृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति। भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया’ (भ. गी. १८। ६१)](http://advaitasharada.sringeri.net/display/bhashya/Gita?page=18&id=BG_C18_V61&hl=%20%E0%A4%88%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%83%20%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%82%20%E0%A4%B9%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%BD%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%A8%20%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A0%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BF%C2%A0%E0%A5%A4%20%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%82%E0%A4%A2%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%20%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE)इति ; सूत्रकारोऽपि परमार्थाभिप्रायेण ‘ तदनन्यत्वम्’ इत्याह ; व्यवहाराभिप्रायेण तु ‘ स्याल्लोकवत्’ इति महासमुद्रस्थानीयतां ब्रह्मणः कथयति, अप्रत्याख्यायैव कार्यप्रपञ्चं परिणामप्रक्रियां चाश्रयति सगुणेषूपासनेषूपयोक्ष्यत इति ॥ १४ ॥

Here Shankara says that the bheda between Ishwara and jiva as the one who impels and the other that is impelled is only vyvaharika and not paramarthika. Here too, the Ishwara is not any deity but the Pure Consciousness alone.  
For correspondence with Ishwarah sarvabhutanam bhashyam of BG with Br.Up.bhashyam: It is interesting to note that Shankara reiterates theदारुकृतपुरुषादीनियन्त्रारूढानि//’machine-like man’ etc., made of wood, mounted on a machine.// analogy he has given in the BG 18.61 bhashya, in a few other places too:बृहदारण्यकोपनिषद्भाष्यम्तृतीयोऽध्यायःचतुर्थं ब्राह्मणम्मन्त्र १– भाष्यम्  

………यो व्यानेन व्यानीतीति — छान्दसं दैर्घ्यम् । सर्वाः कार्यकरणसङ्घातगताः प्राणनादिचेष्टा दारुयन्त्रस्येव येन क्रियन्ते — न हि चेतनावदनधिष्ठितस्य दारुयन्त्रस्येव प्राणनादिचेष्टा विद्यन्ते ; तस्मात् विज्ञानमयेनाधिष्ठितं विलक्षणेन दारुयन्त्रवत् प्राणनादिचेष्टां प्रतिपद्यते — तस्मात् सोऽस्ति कार्यकरणसङ्घातविलक्षणः, यश्चेष्टयति ॥ ………

Here Shankara says that ‘in the absence of Consciousness presiding, the activity of breathing, etc. like a wooden apparatus/contrivance is presided by a sentient person different from the apparatus, cannot be explained. So, the impelling entity is Conscousness, Shankara avers, and is not any deity.

#### बृहदारण्यकोपनिषद्भाष्यम्तृतीयोऽध्यायःसप्तमं ब्राह्मणम्मन्त्र १– भाष्यम्

………लोकं सर्वाणि च भूतानि यः अन्तरः अभ्यन्तरः सन् यमयति नियमयति, दारुयन्त्रमिव भ्रामयति, स्वं स्वमुचितव्यापारं कारयतीति । सोऽब्रवीदेवमुक्तः पतञ्जलः काप्यः —

Here it is the case of antaryami, the internal impellers, who impels the beings like a wooden machine is activated. It is in this context, in the next second mantra bhashyam that Shankara says:

देवताकार्यकरणस्य ईश्वरसाक्षिमात्रसान्निध्येन हि नियमेन प्रवृत्तिनिवृत्ती स्याताम् ; य ईदृगीश्वरो नारायणाख्यः, पृथिवीं पृथिवीदेवताम् , यमयति नियमयति स्वव्यापारे, अन्तरः अभ्यन्तरस्तिष्ठन् , एष त आत्मा, ते तव, मम च सर्वभूतानां च इत्युपलक्षणार्थमेतत् , अन्तर्यामी यस्त्वया पृष्टः, अमृतः सर्वसंसारधर्मवर्जित इत्येतत् ॥

The body-organs of the deity (prithvi) act by the mere presence of the Ishwara-witness. Such an Ishwara (who is a mere witness), called Narayana, impels the prithvi deity, residing within the prithvi deity, is the Self of you and me and all other beings. This is the antaryami that you asked about, is the one that is bereft of all samsara characteristics. Here too Shankara uses the word Narayana, just like in the BG 18.61 bhashya. And also says that the antaryami is a mere witness-presence. That means, it does nothing to impel the body-mind complex of all beings. The impelling takes place by default by the mere proximity of the Consciousness to the presence of the inert body-mind complexes.

Now, at the end of that long section on Antaryami, 3.7.23, Shankara concludes the topic:

अन्यो नियन्ता अन्तर्यामीति प्राप्तम् ; तदन्यत्वाशङ्कानिवृत्त्यर्थमुच्यते — नान्योऽतः — नान्यः — अतः अस्मात् अन्तर्यामिणः नान्योऽस्ति द्रष्टा ; तथा नान्योऽतोऽस्ति श्रोता ; नान्योऽतोऽस्ति मन्ता ; नान्योऽतोऽस्ति विज्ञाता। यस्मात्परो नास्ति द्रष्टा श्रोता मन्ता विज्ञाता, यः अदृष्टो द्रष्टा, अश्रुतः श्रोता, अमतो मन्ता, अविज्ञातो विज्ञाता, अमृतः सर्वसंसारधर्मवर्जितः सर्वसंसारिणां कर्मफलविभागकर्ता — एष ते आत्मा अन्तर्याम्यमृतः ; अस्मादीश्वरादात्मनोऽन्यत् आर्तम्। ततो होद्दालक आरुणिरुपरराम ॥

Objection: The impeller is different from the impelled. Thus the two, the jiva and Ishwara are different.

Reply: The mantra itself says: There is none other than this antaryami who is the seer, hearer, thinker, knower.. He is the unheard-of hearer, unseen seer, etc. He is Immortal, free of all samsaric traits but is the bestower of the fruits of karma to the samsarins. Such a one is your Self, the antaryami. Everything that is other than this Ishwara, the Atman, is miserable.

From this statement of Shankara, and the Upanishad, we conclude:

- The Ishwara, impeller of BG 18.61, stated as Narayana by Shankara, is
  the very Atman, the Pure Consciousness.
- The wooden machine, like a puppet, is a fine analogy for demonstrating
  the need for a sentient impeller.
- The places where the BG 18.61 are cited by Shankara are clearly
  jiva-Ishwara abheda teaching ones.
- This can’t be an identity between a deity and the jiva; it can only be
  involving Pure Consciousness.
- The Br.Up. Antaryami Brahmanam is nothing but an elaboration of the BG
  18.61.
- The Antaryami is Pure consciousness, which really does not impel but
  by its mere presence, the organs get impelled.
- The Kenopanishad ‘shrotrasya shrotram,..’ etc. is what is taught
  here.
- The expression सर्वसंसारिणां कर्मफलविभागकर्ताin the above bhashyam is the
  show, like ‘Kshetrajnam chaapi maam viddhi’ of the Gita, that the Self
  of all is that sentient being (that has to be admitted for karma is
  jaDa and requires a sentient Supreme to give the phala to the
  kartrus. 

Thus, from the above study we can conclude that the ‘tam’ (him) in ‘tameva sharanam gaccha..’ of the BG 18.62 refers to the Antaryami, Pure Consciousness.

Om Tat Sat

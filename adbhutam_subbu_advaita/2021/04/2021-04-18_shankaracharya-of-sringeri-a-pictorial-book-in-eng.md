Shankaracharya of Sringeri (A pictorial book in English, in comic style) –Free download of e-book

Sri Adi Shankara Bhagavatpada, the celebrated exponent of Advaita-vedanta who is revered as an incarnation of Lord Shiva, founded the esteemed Sringeri Sri Sharada Peetham. It has been headed by an unbroken line of enlightened, monastic, compassionate gurus. This 32-page book in comic style, Shankaracharya of Sringeri, contains lifelike illustrations depicting the inspiring biography and teachings of His Holiness Jagadguru Sri Bharathi Theertha Mahaswamiji, the 36th Shankaracharya and reigning pontiff of Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham.  
“Will God appear to us, humans? Is God partial? How does He grace one?
What should one earn and save for oneself?” The Jagadguru’s insightful answers for questions such as these have been interestingly presented in this book, in a style that would appeal to both children and adults alike.

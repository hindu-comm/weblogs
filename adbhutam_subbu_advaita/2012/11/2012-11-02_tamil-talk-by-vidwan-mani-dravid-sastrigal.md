Namaste.

During the recent Vidwat Sadas in the presence of the Sringeri Acharya Dr. Mani Dravid SastrinaH delivered a talk on the ‘AnandamayAdhikaraNam’ of the Brahmasutra bhashyam. This talk was in Sanskrit. Now the eminent scholar, upon request by some of those who wanted the contents of the talk rendered in Tamil, has done so and this recording is now uploaded for which a link is provided below.

**Q: I would really like to know your story, by which I mean how did you think to connect Land to Hinduism and how one might go about reading Land to appreciate your interpretation of his work?**

A: My first introduction to Land was probably the videos by Charlemagne talking about *The Dark Enlightenment* years ago. After that, I found the Skeptical Waves channel and started listening to his TTS archives, including those of Nick Land's writings. The idea to connect Land/Acc to Hinduism was a **very** recent one (as in it probably sprouted up in my head around Jan/Feb. I can't tell you the exact moment or reason it happened but I think I was listening to Land’s (2017) *A Quick-And-Dirty Introduction To Accelerationism* again when the inspiration hit.

I'm pretty sure it was when I heard this:

> "In philosophical terms, the deep problem of acceleration is
> transcendental. It describes an absolute horizon – and one that is
> closing in. Thinking takes time, and accelerationism suggests we’re
> running out of time to think that through…"

When I heard that, the first thing that popped up in my head was the phrase "Hindu rate of growth", which I mention towards the beginning of my introduction.

Initially, my plan was merely to attempt to understand Accelerationism because my first impression of it was a rather distasteful one. I took (and am still taking) copious amounts of physical notes trying to understand the corpus of Land's early work. After I got deeper and deeper into it though, the things that I thought were merely coincidental similarities to Hindu ideas became far too frequent for me to ignore and I adjusted my plan.

**Q: Is your goal with this "metaphysics of Land and Hinduism" ultimately connected to some nationalist project?**

A: No. In fact, I'd say it's quite the opposite. To keep it simple, I don't believe in any mainstream HNat movement or org that I know of at the moment and I think the idea of nationalism runs counter to the landian acc ethos, esp. in its contemporary neoliberal iterations.

**Q: Is your thesis centered around the fact that the modernity brought about by the Kali Yuga is inevitable and should be accelerated?**

A: Not entirely. The Landian Accelerationist position is that capitalism is the driver of change and makes itself real by causing the past from the future in an inversion of commonly understood causality. This in my view actually has no (metaphysical) problems from a Hindu perspective and should merely be understood as a lesser unfolding of God’s Will within the cycle of history that it will last for, however long that may be.

In regards to humans accelerating the process, the only thing we can do is to get out of the way. I have a few ideas in mind for how we should respond to that but you’ll have to wait for future posts to learn more ;)

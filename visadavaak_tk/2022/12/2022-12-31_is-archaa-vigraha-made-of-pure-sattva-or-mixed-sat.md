viśadavaak

Q: Is archaa vigraha made of pure sattva? Or mixed sattva?

A: Archaa vigraha is pure sattva, no doubt. Although Bhagavan accepts as His divine body – a form made out of prAkRta vastu such as gold, silver, brass, copper, wood, stone etc., He resides in those forms with His shuddhasattva tirumeni. The rajas and tamas in that material are removed, and sattva alone remains.

In his book tattvatrayam, Pillai lokacharyar says, “archAvatAramAvadu – tamarugandadevvuruvam engirapaDiyE cEtanarkkabhimatamAna dravyattilE…. sannidhi paNNi….kOyilgaLilum gRuhangaLilum ezhundaruLi niRkkum nilai” (அர்ச்சாவதாரமாவது – தமருகந்ததெவ்வுருவம் என்கிரபடியே சேதநர்க்கபிமதமான த்ரவ்யத்திலே…. ஸந்நிதி பண்ணி….கோயில்களிலும் க்ருஹங்களிலும் எழுன்தருளி நிற்க்கும் நிலை).

Here, he quotes Poigai azhwar’s pasuram “tamarugandadevvuruvam” – meaning, Bhagavan takes that very form which his dear devotees rejoice. In the commentary, Periyavacchan Pillai gives examples of gold, silver and other materials used for making His forms. He says, Bhagavan happily accepts such forms and resides in them.

However, there is no explicit statement as to whether He resides in those forms alongside rajas and tamas, or takes that as His own shuddhasattva tirumeni. Some scholars opine that He does not alter the material constituents, and resides in those forms alongside rajas and tamas. That rajas and tamas in that divine form are used to conceal Bhagavan from theives, etc.

But that is not completely accepted.

Bhagavan is identified with a unique qualifier called ‘ubhayalinga-vaishishtyam’. That is to say, He possesses two forms – namely akhilaheyapratibhatatvam and kalyanaikataanatvam.  
Akhila = all  
heya = blemishes  
pratibhata = opposer (remover)

kalyana = auspicious qualities  
ekataana = the sole residence.

So, as you see here, Bhagavan is the one who is not just free from blemishes Himself, but also removes the blemishes of all those connected with Him. He is also the sole residence for all the auspicious qualities. In that sense, He is the one who removes the rajas and tamas from all that is connected with Him. Therefore, He removes the rajas and tamas from the archaa forms as well.

But this cannot be a statement without basis. So we have to now establish why this has to be accepted. Let us examine in the forthcoming posts.

**Raping India's Forests — The highest Point of Political "Hindutva"**

**Shrikant G. Talageri**



I had thought of giving this article the title "….**The Highest Point of RSS 'Hindutva'**", but realized that would be shifting the blame from the tree to the branch. It is the fashion to consider the BJP to be a wing of the RSS and under its control, but it has been decades now since I have realized that it is the opposite way round (and many people who were initially sceptical have now come round to my point of view): the BJP is not the "political wing" of the RSS; the RSS (even though it was technically born decades earlier) is the "recruitment wing" of the BJP.

From the first inklings of the recent political crisis in Maharashtra, which led to the toppling of the SS-Cong-NCA alliance government, I had predicted that the first thing the BJP would do as soon as it came to power would be letting loose the cannons of war on the Aarey forest in Mumbai. After all, the successful midnight slaughter of over 2000 Aarey trees is one (and the first) of the only two times that we witnessed "midnight coups" in Maharashtra in the last few years. \[The second, people who accuse the Shiv Sena of betraying the voters by aligning with the Congress-NCP conveniently forget, is when the BJP made a failed midnight attempt to install a BJP-NCP government in the state to avoid giving Chief Ministership to the Shiv Sena — a contingency which they do not seem to have been able to avoid this time round. But then the "Balasaheb Shiv Sena", not exactly a part of either the BJP or the RSS, seems no different from the BJP in their lust to attack India's forests, as today's first decision of the new Maharashtra government shows; so my choice of the word "*political* 'Hindutva'" in the title was right: neither the term "BJP" nor "RSS" Hindutva would have covered them\].

This (***except in the Aarey matter***) is not in support of the toppled Shiv Sena government. The BJP and the Shiv Sena (after the demise of the second Hindu Hriday Samrat, Bal Thackeray, the first being Veer Savarkar) were the tweedledum and tweedledee of Pseudo-Hindutva
politics in India. The Shiv Sena government, whose attitude during the sadhu-slaughter episode in Palghar can never be forgotten, and the statement of the Pramukh of whose Dakshin Mumbai Vibhag (that the sound of the *azaan* is the sweetest sound he has ever heard, and that, every time an *azaan* ends, he eagerly awaits the next one — a blatantly false statement at which many Muslims of my acquaintance also could not refrain from snickering) also comes to mind, was noted for its sharp swing away from Hindutva and towards "Secularism", and its record, even allowing for the covid-and-lockdown interlude, was a dismal one. And, in his zeal to make the party and government a captive plaything in the hands of his son, Uddhav Thackeray made the same mistake that the BJP had earlier made: the BJP had taken the support of the Shiv Sena for granted ("TINA - where else will they go? What else can they do?") and often downgraded and sidelined them for more than five years, and Thackeray did the same to the other Shiv Sena leaders.



But the BJP in the last eight years has stabbed Hindutva in the back in countless lethal ways. To enumerate just a *very* *few*:

1. They redefined who is a "true Hindu" and what is true Hindutva on the basis of Islam and Muslims: “**The day it is said that we don’t want Muslims, Hindutva will cease to exist**” (RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat), or (the following quote is something I saw somewhere, but failed to note the exact source, and if I am right, I will welcome someone telling me the source, and if I am wrong, I will welcome correction) "**A Hindu who hates Islam is not a true Hindu**" (PM Modi).

2. They redefined Hindu prayers: many of the "minority" wings of the RSS and BJP have been doing this for years, but the one that comes to mind immediately is the BJP leader Piyush Goyal who "revealed" a few years ago that he recites the *kalma* ***la*** ***ilaha*** ***illalla***, ***mohammadun*** ***rasulallah*** in his daily prayers. Clearly a mullah must reveal to him what Islamic law prescribes for someone who recites the *kalma* and thereby becomes a Muslim, and then apostasizes by praying to other Gods *even* *once*, *let* *alone* *on* *a* *daily* *basis*.

3. They refused outright to give Hindu religious and other institutions the same autonomy that Muslim and Christian religious and other institutions enjoy in India, and then looted all the wealth and properties of Hindu temples and maṭhs (this was a major activity in Rajasthan under the Vasundhara Raje government) and used the funds (and other Hindu taxpayers' funds) to promote massive *minorities*-*only* schemes for Muslims and Christians to the tune, in monetary terms alone, of **more** **than* *70000* *crore* *rupees**. This is just the tip of the iceberg.

4. They showed full sympathy for the hurt feelings of "minorities" (including *Obama* when he complained about minorities in India being persecuted!) whenever the opportunity arose, then turned a blind eye when non-Hindus abused Hinduism in the dirtiest possible terms, expressed full sympathies when minorities and anti-Hindus suffered anything (even, as in the case of Shabana Azmi, a minor injury in an accident), and turned a completely blind eye and deaf ear when Hindus were given death threats and even killed in the most gruesome ways (the sadhus in Palgar, Kamlesh Tiwari, Kishan Bharwad, Kanhaiya Lal, etc. etc.).

What happened to Noopur Sharma (and in thousands of other such unpublicized cases) is not new or unique. A few years ago, Koenraad Elst, who had been invited to an RSS sponsored Seminar in Goa, was woken up in the middle of the night and bundled back home to Belgium (although he was supposed to be at the Seminar till it ended) because an Arab Shaikh who was a chief guest at the seminar had objected to something he said in his talk on that day!!



But, instead of wallowing in all the all dirt dug up by them, let me come to the highest point of their "Hindutva": it is the rape and gang rape of India's forests, trees, ecology and environmental heritage. If Mother India is Draupadi, the BJP leaders have shown themselves to be Kauravas. While all the above points deal with Hindutva matters about which "Hindutva" leaders don't really care two figs either way, except at the time of elections (or of disputes with *other* "Hindutva" leaders), the mass destruction of India's forests is a hardcore part of their true ideology, which is "**Money**". It is in the destruction of forests, grabbing of forest lands (and not just *forest* lands), and "development" (not just only in the grabbed forest lands, but anywhere where there is land of any kind), which provides the most massive, certain and inexhaustible source of wealth for politicians, and in the case of the politicians who ask for votes in the name of Hindu issues, it is this wealth which represents their "Hindutva" ideology. And it is not just forest land in mainland India: in the Andaman islands, natives of the islands, who have lived in those areas for *over* *50000* *years*, are being evacuated from their ancestral areas and herded into camps so that their lands can be used for making massive amounts of money in the name of "development" (and, in order to facilitate this, they started out by opening up reserved parts of the islands to foreigners with vested interests, including missionaries).

Let me add: there are **absolutely** **countless** committed monkeys on the internet (not all of them necessarily members of the trolling brigades of the party) who will be jumping up and down and clapping their hands in glee at all this "development", and I can expect to have them viciously snapping at my heels and spouting vitriol over what I am writing here. There is always a heavy price to pay for speaking the truth\!

I remember, on a visit to the RSS center at Uttan in the Bhayandar area to the immediate north of Mumbai in 2018 (the occasion was the sixth International Conference and Gathering of the Elders — an important confluence of members of Pagan religions worldwide), an RSS functionary was pointing out to some eminent visitors that people in Mumbai were facing the continuous inconveniences of major "development" works (the Metros, Coastal Roads, etc. which seem to have turned most parts of Mumbai into veritable ruins in the last many years) without a murmur of protest because they knew that all this was for their ultimate benefit.

"**Does India include its Hindu Citizens or not**?"

This is the question assailing my mind when I read absolutely diametrically opposite reports about whether India's image or India's importance in the world is on the *rise* or on the *wane*.

*On the one hand*, we have Modi-bhakts of every variety insisting that under Modi India has become one of the most important countries in the world, that Indophobic elements are now wary of attacking India because it is now too important a country economically, technologically and politically for the world to continue to treat it with the same contempt or disdain with which it used to treat India in the past, because of the strong personality now ruling India and the strong foreign policies being followed by the present government.

But wait, it is not only "Modi-bhakts" who say this. There are any number of experts and spokespersons all over the world who now emphasize the growing importance of India as an international force to be reckoned with. I need give no examples to substantiate this: BJP supporters on the social media regularly provide all the international testimonials to this effect. It only happened to be strongly brought to my notice today when I saw that even agenda-based media like Al-Jazeera concede this fact. In an article dated today, 21 September 2023, titled "**Hardeep Singh Nijjar killing row live: India defends visa suspension move**", the writer concedes this fact:

[https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2023/9/21/canada-india-tensions-live-updates-visa-services-for-canadians-suspended](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2023/9/21/canada-india-tensions-live-updates-visa-services-for-canadians-suspended)

"**Despite Canada’s explosive allegations that India may have been involved in the killing of a Canadian citizen on Canadian soil, its closest allies have expressed** [muted reaction about what happened](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/9/21/why-canada-lacks-allies-support-on-claim-india-killed-hardeep-singh-nijjar)**.**

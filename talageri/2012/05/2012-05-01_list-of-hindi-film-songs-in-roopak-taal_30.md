List of Hindi Film Songs in Roopak Taal of 7 beats

The most uncommon taal in Hindi film songs is the Jhap Taal of 10 beats. I have already given a more or less exhaustive list of Hindi film songs in Jhap Taal. The second most uncommon taal is Roopak Taal, based on a rhythmic cycle of 7 beats. Some of the songs may be in other taals (Jhumra, Deepchandi, Dhamar, Aadachautaal, etc.) based on the same rhythmic cycle of 7 beats or multiples of 7 (mainly 14).

  

I present here a more or less exhaustive list of *124 Hindi film songs in Roopak Taal and other Taals of the same rhythmic cycle* (of which two songs, from Arzoo and Zindagi Aur Maut, are repeated in the list as male and female versions respectively).

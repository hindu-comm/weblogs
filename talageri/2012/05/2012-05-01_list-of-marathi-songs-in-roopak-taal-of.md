**List of Marathi Songs in Roopak Taal**

  

**30/4/2012 (Updated 26/7/2020)**

  

This is a list of Marathi songs (film songs, bhav geet, bhakti geet, natya geet) in taals with a rhythmic cycle of 7 beats or multiples (14). This is mainly Roopak Taal, but the list may include songs in other taals which fall in this category, like Deepchandi, Dhamar, Jhumra and Aadachautaal.

List of Holidays in Indian Consulate Office in New York for the Year 2021

I was going through some old handwritten notes of mine, and came across a "List of Holidays in the Consulate General's Office in New York" in some **old** (Congress) year. Interestingly, this was highlighted in an article published in the BJP mouthpiece "Organiser" (frankly, I do not know the year and date of the issue of the magazine) to point out how Hindu holidays were of no importance to the Indian Consulate in new York representing a Congress government in Delhi.  

Just as a matter of interest, before disposing of my old handwritten notes, I decided to check the list of holidays in the Consulate General of India's office in new York for the present year (2021) under a Organiser-backed "pro-Hindu" Indian government. I find *17 holidays*, and, on checking further, I find that this list also applies to the Consulate General of India's office in Chicago for the year 2021 as well as the previous year 2020. I did not bother to check any further.

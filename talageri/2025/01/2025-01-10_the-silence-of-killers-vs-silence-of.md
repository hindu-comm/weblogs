Is it worth an article? Well, my childhood ambition always was to be a journalist, or more specifically a “column” writer in some magazine or journal who, as a regular columnist, could always set down his thoughts in print on any and every subject as whim took him. I never became a journalist, but then isn’t a blog also a medium to express one’s feelings on any and every subject as one’s whim or mood dictates? So, my thoughts on this vague-seeming topic: **The Silence of the Killers vs. The Silence of the Dead**

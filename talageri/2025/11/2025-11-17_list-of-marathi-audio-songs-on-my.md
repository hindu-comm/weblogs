**LIST OF MARATHI AUDIO SONGS ON MY COMPUTER**

**Shrikant G. Talageri**





I have a massive collection of songs in my computer: mainly Hindi and Marathi, video and audio, systematically arranged in folders and files, but also not-so-well-arranged Classical (Hindustani and Carnatic) and assorted semiclassical songs and folk songs (in other languages as well). This is apart from three cupboards full of my favorite books, mainly novels and storybooks. They are the solace of my life.

But I have recently become bitten by the uploading itch: I feel that one never knows when the end will come, and all this wealth of music and books will become redundant. So (senseless though it may be) I feel I should at least upload *lists* of the songs in my computer for the record. If nothing else, people interested in old Hindi and Marathi songs (there are *very* few new ones in my lists) can at least go through the lists and see the names and titles of singers and songs that they used to hear in the past but whose existence they may have almost forgotten, and perhaps this may kindle nostalgic and happy memories. \[Actually I do have bigger collections of more obscure songs, mostly audios, but they are not-so-well-arranged\].

At any rate, any itch begs to be scratched: so here is my first and easiest-to-prepare-and-upload list: a **list of the Marathi Audio Songs on my computer**:

\[The number 3/ 5/ 7 after any song represents the number of beats in the **tāl** of that song.

Spelling mistakes, etc. should be excused: I do not have the patience to sit down and correct all the lists

Likewise, if any song is repeated in the lists, I am sorry for that\].

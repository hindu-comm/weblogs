Listen to a Panorama of Popular Hindi Solo Songs by Female Singers



**Listen to a Panorama of Popular Hindi Solo Songs by Female Singers**

**Shrikant G. Talageri**



\[*Note 1*: This includes mainly **solo** Hindi **film** **songs** (but also quite a few **non**-**film** **songs** and **semi-classical thumris** and **bhajans**) by **Female** **Singers**, ***other than*** the two most prolific singers **Asha Bhosle** and **Lata** **Mangeshkar**.

It must be noted that some of the lesser-known of these singers (and others not in this list) have **duet** **songs** or **multisinger** **songs** which are more well-known or popular than their **solos**, but those **duet** **songs** and **multisinger** **songs** are not included in this list.

*Note 2*: All the songs may not be there below, either because I am unaware of others, or because they are not available, or because they are obscure ones that I do not consider particularly worthy of including in the lists below. Needless to say, *countless songs, from the sea of **Hindi** **songs**, will be missing in the collection below, and newer songs and singers especially *will* mostly be missing in the lists*\].

It must be noted that many more songs of each of the singers may be found on youtube. I have only given the main ones that I have known and liked. Actually, not all are liked by me: in fact two of them (**Tum** **Meri** **Rakho** **Laaj** **Hari** by **Sudha** **Malhotra** from **Dekh** **Kabira** **Roya**, and **Khuli** **Hai** **Ankh** by **Suman** **Kalyanpur** from **Miya** **Biwi** **Razi**), are songs which particularly irritate me, because both of them are really *beautiful* songs, which are (in my opinion at least) *completely* *spoilt* by the raucous “comedy” interpolations within the songs. I know there were versions without these raucous interpolations, since I remember hearing them on the radio long ago; but those are not available any more. I wonder that the great music directors who composed these songs (**Madanmohan** and **SD** **Burman** respectively) did not insist on getting the pure songs (without the “comedy” bits) separately recorded for music lovers!

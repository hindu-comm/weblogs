**Should Akhilesh Yadav Apologize for the Killing of Kar-Sewaks?**

**Shrikant G. Talageri**



I stopped reading newspapers and watching the news channels since 2019, because I realized what was going to be happening in India thenceforth. However, I am constantly on the internet, and often important news items do catch my eye. yesterday I had been to the house of a (Modi-bhakt) friend in whose house news channels are continuously running whole day long. The particular channel was **India** **TV**, and the hot news going on and on was about **Akhilesh** **Yadav** (the chief of the **Samajwadi** **Party**) apparently going to be going for ***darshan*** at the **Ram** **Janmabhoomi** **Mandir** **Ayodhya** on **12^(th)** **December** **2026**. The channel was agog with reaction to this news from all sides (with spokespersons and journalists from the **BJP**, **AIMIM** and **SP** constantly stuck on the screen).



The headlines flashing across the screen all the time mainly spoke about the fact thay **Akhilesh** **Yadav's**father, **Mulayam** **Singh** **Yadav**, as the then **Chief** **Minister** of **Uttar** **Pradesh** had ordered firing on **kar**-**sewaks** in **Ayodhya** on **30**/**10**/**1990** and **2**/**11**/**1990**: the official death tally was **15** (but the **VHP** claims to have identified **59** victims). The headlines asked poignant questions:

“**Do** **nāv** **mein** **savārī** … **kyā** **akhileś** **ko** **paḍegī** **bhārī**?” (“**With one foot each in two boats … will it cost Akhilesh heavily?**”),

“**Akhileś kā jai śrī rām… ayodhyā karegā viśvās?**” (“**Will Ayodhya trust Akhilesh saying ‘Jay Shri Ram’?**”)

And “**apne mahāpāp ke liye māfī māṅgiye**” (“**Apologize for your Great Sin**”):

**BJP** **spokespersons** repeatedly demanded that **Akhilesh** apologize for ***his*** ***father’s*** **Great** **Sin** before daring to think of entering the **Ram** **Temple**.



Two general points:

1. Any **Hindu** can visit the **Ayodhya** temple. \[So can **non**-**Hindus**, if they do so with respect, but that is a secondary issue\].

2. It is **Akhilesh’s** ***father*** who ordered the firings, not **Akhilesh** himself. It is an ***Abrahamic*** idea that ***descendants must pay for the sins of their fathers***. While ***Akhilesh*** is also a multiple-times sinner against **Hinduism**, he is in no way himself responsible for the firings which killed the **kar**-**sewaks**.



But should **Akhilesh** apologize for his father’s actions anyway?

***Yes****, but only if **Narendra** **Modi**, and the BJP as a **party**, first apologize for rewarding **Mulayam** **Singh** for ordering the **firing** on **kar**-**sewaks**, and for simultaneously **insulting** the **martyred** **kar**-**sewaks** and treating them like dirt, by awarding the **Padma** **Vibhushan** award to **Mulayam** **Singh** **Yadav** (post-humously) on **April** **5** **2023***. It was ultimately on the foundation laid on the corpses of those martyrs (and others before and after them) that the BJP shot up from **2** seats in the **Lok** **Sabha** in **1984** to **353** seatsin **2019**.

Much can be written and said on all the different aspects of the matter, But this is the only fundamental point I find necessary to make on the subject.

The whole ***govardhan*** ***parvata*** incident very likely represents a memory of an event where a section of **Yadus** at a particular point resisted the enforced worship of the **Pūru** **God** **Indra** in place of their own ***govardhan*** ***parvata***, and provides *recorded* *evidence* (at the very starting point of this process, in **western** **Uttar** **Pradesh**) of the whole process by which the **Pūru** **Vedic** **religion** spread *all* *over* *India*, incorporated *all* *the* *local* *religious* *elements* *from* *all* *over* *India* within itself (to the extent that those borrowed elements more and more came to replace the elements of the original **Pūru** **Vedic** **religion** as the dominant features of the spreading religion), with the elements of the original **Pūru** **Vedic** **religion** finally remaining only as the nominal (or elite) “**pan-Indian umbrella of Hinduism**”.

***Can anyone deny the power and beauty of the way in which this song expresses its love for India (note especially the underlined words)***?



But few people know the other side of this story (i.e. of **Iqbal**): the above song “**Sare** **Jahan** **Se** **Achha**" was written in **1904** and first published in the weekly journal “**Ittehad**” on **August** **16**, **1904**. Clearly Iqbal was a passionate and heartfelt patriot at that time.

However, within a few years, he turned into a fanatical **Jihadi** and **Islamist**, and in **1908**/**1910** he penned a ***completely new poem*** expressing his ***new ideology***. This song was “**Chin-o-Arab Hamara**”. In **1910**, he wrote *"**Tarana-e-Milli**"*, declaring that the **global Muslim community** (***Ummah***) transcended **geography**. *The opening lines changed from loving **one’s** **country** to claiming the entire world for **Islam***:

**Iqbal’s** incredible and total transformation is expressed succinctly in the following **Google** **AI** **Overview** reply to the question: “**Iqbal as a proponent of Pakistan**”:

“Allama Muhammad Iqbal provided the core philosophical and ideological foundation for the creation of Pakistan by articulating the **Two**-**Nation** **Theory** advocating for a consolidated Muslim-majority region in northwestern India.

**The 1930 Allahabad Address**: In his presidential address to the All-India Muslim League, Iqbal formally proposed the creation of a unified North-West Indian Muslim state—incorporating the Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan—stating that the destiny of Muslims was best served in an autonomous home within or outside the British Empire.

**Philosophical Separation**: Rejecting western secular nationalism, he argued that Islam was not just a private faith but a social and political order capable of unifying the community (***Ummah***) and safeguarding **Muslim** **identity** from domination in a **Hindu**-**majority** **India**.

**Mentorship of Jinnah**: During the **1930s**, **Iqbal** wrote critical letters to **Muhammad** **Ali** **Jinnah** urging him to return from London to lead the **All**-**India** **Muslim** **League** and mobilize the Muslim masses toward the realization of this **separate** **homeland**.”

**Iqbal** (who died in **1938** in **Lahore**) was honored posthumously as ***Mufakkir-e-Pakistan*** ("**The Thinker of Pakistan**") and the **National** **Poet** **of** **Pakistan**.

***In short***: No-one can deny that “**Sare Jahan Se Accha**” is an *extremely beautiful song and must become well-known to every Indian as one of the great patriotic songs of India*.

But it also has another and perhaps more important and useful purpose. The full history of this song, **and its aftermath**, should be known to every Indian as a reminder of how the **religious** **fanaticism** and **hatred**-**of**-**other**-**religions** preached by **Abrahamic** **religions** can transform the writer of a song like “**Sare Jahan Se Accha**” into a **hater** **of** **India** who mentored a **Jinnah**, also originally a **secularist** until he also got transformed into a (at least **Political**) **Islamist** and declared: “**We will either have a divided India or a destroyed India**”.

**Iqbal** and his two poems are the most classical examples of the powerful transformative abilities of **hate**-**ideologies** like **Islam** and **Evangelist** **Christianity**.

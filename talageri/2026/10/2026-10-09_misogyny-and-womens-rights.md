That “positive measures” to achieve “positive discrimination” for women sometimes goes berserk is beyond doubt: people (whether out of excessive zeal born out of deep convictions, or purely out of ulterior motives) do often tend to go to extremes in what they do in certain matters. But positive measures are very definitely needed to make the lives of women less of a hell, or more worth living. And it is necessary to understand exactly how much of a great need there *is* for pro-women measures to be rigorously pursued.

Women constitute, to put it simplistically (since the ratio is never exactly fifty-fifty) **Half of Humankind**. How does this **Half of Humankind** fare in human society?

**A**. **The Work of Nature or “God”.**

Let me begin by stating that it is not just *human* *society* which has contributed to it being a punishment to be a woman in this human world: **Nature** (or “**God**”) has contributed to a more fundamental extent than human beings themselves:

To start first of all with the most glaringly prominent and blatant aspects, women suffer from four very troublesome and *fundamental* physical handicaps, disadvantages or problems that *men* cannot even *begin* to understand, since *they* do not face these problems:

1. Menstruation and Periods, with all the attendant inconveniences.

2. Many months of tiring Pregnancy.

3. Painful Childbirth.

4. Menopause and the physical and mental ailments associated with it.

In my opinion, these four things alone justify women being given special privileges over men, to compensate the injustice of Nature. Incidentally, that most people, including women themselves, would probably not exactly treat the above points (especially the second and third) as *disadvantages*, but as something special and blessed, is a different matter. But I know that *I* consider myself lucky that *I* am not “blessed” in these ways!



But there is more. A search on the internet produced the following list of additional physical handicaps:

**“Muscle Strength and Mass**

**Upper body strength:** Women generally have less total muscle mass and lower average upper-body and handgrip strength compared to men.

***Muscle growth*:** Female bodies typically experience less muscle hypertrophy (enlargement of muscle fibers) in response to resistance training.

**Cardiovascular and Respiratory Capacity**

***Heart size*:** A woman’s heart is typically smaller than a man's heart, which influences blood flow volume and circulation dynamics.

***Oxygen carrying*:** Women generally have a reduced oxygen-carrying capacity in the blood because of lower average hemoglobin levels and smaller lung volumes.

**Bone Density and Joint Health**

***Bone mass*:** Women start life with a lower peak bone mass and smaller bones than men.

***Osteoporosis*:** The drop in the hormone estrogen during menopause accelerates bone loss, making women significantly more prone to osteoporosis (brittle, porous bones) and related fractures.

***Osteoarthritis*:** Women experience higher rates of osteoarthritis in the knees, hips, and hands as they age.

**Anatomical and Infection Risks**

***Urinary tract infections (UTIs)*:** Women have a much shorter urethra (the tube for passing urine) that sits close to bacteria sources, making bladder infections much more common than in men.

***Sexually transmitted infections (STIs)*:** Female urogenital anatomy features a larger and more delicate mucosal surface area, which can increase biological vulnerability to certain bacterial and viral transmissions”.



For some reason (perhaps because I knew female lions did most of the hunting) I had thought that this *physical* weakness at least was there only among human beings, and this was perhaps not necessarily so in the animal world as a whole.

However, a deeper examination of the subject brought the following to my notice (in respect of lions and lionesses to begin with):

Male lions are larger, stronger, and more powerful than lionesses.

**Size and Weight:** Adult male lions weigh between 330 and 550 pounds, while lionesses weigh between 265 and 400 pounds.

**Muscle and Power:** Males have heavier bone structures, thicker necks, and massive muscles built for fighting rival males.

**Manes:** Males grow thick hair manes that protect their necks from sharp teeth and claws during fights.

Lionesses do most of the hunting (about 90%) apparently not because they are more physically powerful than male lions, but because they are faster, lighter, and more agile. Male lions do less hunting, but their superior strength allows them to protect the territory, fend off rivals, and steal or defend heavy kills from other predators like hyenas.

In fact, even in the world of animals, birds, reptiles and insects, the only species where females are physically more powerful than the males, are apparently **spotted** **hyenas**, **killer** **whales**, **praying** **mantises**, **black** **widow** **spiders**, **bald** **eagles** and **green** **anacondas** (and perhaps a few rare others)! And in most species, the **females** do not even have the superficial advantage of being considered more spectacular to look at than the **males**: consider the **peacock** and **peahen**!



But it is a fact that women, perhaps **because** of all these natural disadvantages, weaknesses and handicaps, are generally the stronger of the two genders: *their capacity to bear pain, troubles, tensions, uncongenial surroundings, etc. is generally far superior to that of men*. I still remember a quaint way of putting this from the book “**Gone With the Wind**”: “**The man roared like a bull when a splinter was in his finger, and the woman muffled the moans of childbirth, lest she disturb him**”. But then perhaps this innate “strength” is also one which is really a *forced* one: forced on women by *society*?

\[But, for the record, there *are* rare species, e.g. **bees** and **ants**, in which the **females** are not only more strong and powerful (having stingers as weapons which are missing in the males), but are practically the *only* important gender; and **males** are present only for the purpose of mating with the “**queen**” of the colony to further the propagation of the species; and, incredibly, the **males** do not live for long after they complete this duty (such also notoriously being the case with **black** **widow** **spiders**!)\]

This brings us to the multiple ways in which *society* itself has contributed to intensifying and worsening the life of women, already made a punishment by Nature/God!

1. ***Abuse of Special Provisions***:

The first and biggest hazard of acts and laws designed to protect the rights of women is that they can be misused/abused as personal weapons to set the dogs of war on to completely innocent people. Laws to protect women, as I have been emphasizing throughout this article, are absolutely necessary. But equally necessary is the need to protect completely innocent people from becoming the victims of such laws.

I had made this point even in my earlier article “**Rapists, ‘Child Rights’, ‘Left’ and ‘Right’**”:

“**what exactly constitutes rape? This is significant and important, because the fashionable definition of “rape” today fudges and obfuscates the definition to an extent which makes this question not only relevant but crucial to the issue.**



***Note*: the point being made here is *not* about the potential misuse of any potential laws on punishment for rapists. That is, the question here is *not* about whether any particular accusation of rape would be true or false: that is a *different* issue, and the honest and logical conclusion would depend on an examination of the facts and evidence in each individual case separately. Any law can be misused, and fake accusations be made. For example, dalits – that is people belonging to castes which have traditionally been dominated, oppressed, exploited and discriminated against – are indeed still being dominated, oppressed, exploited and discriminated against in many or most parts of the country. But that does not mean that every “dalit” is being so treated, that every “dalit” grievance is genuine, or that every “dalit” accusation is true: there has been widespread blatant and unjustifiable misuse of laws, designed to punish discrimination against dalits, by “dalits” who are in powerful or safe positions, or who seek to make hay while the sun shines and benefit through blackmail and extortion. But they do not negate each other: and *discussion cannot be about the principle as a whole, either way, but about the facts and evidence in each individual case*. Likewise, while rape is a horrible reality, every accusation of rape need not necessarily be true: it could, in individual cases, be a case of malice or blackmail. That question cannot be discussed here *in the abstract*, even while it cannot be used to derail or sabotage stringent laws against genuine rape: *the guilty person in each individual case, whether the genuinely accused or the fraudulent accuser, deserves the most stringent punishment imaginable*.**



**The point being made here is: how do you define rape?**

3. ***The New Form of Dowry: “The Wedding”***:

People may wonder how the issue of **dowry** can become part of the “**Other Side of the Coin**” when I am writing against the iniquities heaped on women by **Human** **Society** and in support of **women’s** **rights**: surely it is still on the **same** **side** **of** **the** **coin** as just *one* *more* of those **iniquities**?

The general verdict on the **Dowry** **System** (which I saw somewhere on the internet) is roughly: “**Marriage turned daughters into *a massive financial liability*. A bride's family was forced to pay hefty dowries (cash, jewelry, goods) to the groom’s family, which further fueled *the perception of daughters as burdens***”.

Surely it is on the **same** side of the coin: as one more of the **injustices** heaped on the **girl** **child**, and the reason for “**the perception of daughters as burdens**”?

Well, in order to understand this, it is necessary to understand what exactly “**dowry**” represented/represents.

Today although **dowry** is as prevalent as ever, the awareness that it is somehow **wrong** (or a “**social** **evil**”) is also present in everyone’s mind. But the point is: *what exactly is it about dowry that makes it **wrong** and a **social** **evil***? Basically, it means the father of the bride giving “**(cash, jewelry, goods) *to the groom’s family***”. The various “**wrong**” aspects of this were:

1. It was not given to the **daughter** herself to be used at her discretion and as per her wishes (which would have been perfectly understandable, since as a **daughter** of the house she would naturally have a right to a share in her father’s wealth) but ***to the*** ***groom** **or** **his** **family to be under their control and discretion***.

2. It was given out of *fear* of possible repercussions ***as per demands from the groom or his family and not as per the father’s own wishes***.

3. It was given ***without reference to, and usually far in excess of, the actual giving capacity and affordability of the father***.



Yes, ***everyone knows the evils of dowry***. And especially in the increasingly growing **educated** and **literate** families, and “modern” families, the outright “dowry” menace may actually have become *less* *than* *earlier*. But in spite of that, *why is it that **a daughter has not become *less* of a “burden” to the family, but probably *much* *more* of a financial burden, making a girl child undesirable and (*in my opinion*) making general evil practices like female foeticide not just understandable but even justifiable in the case of poor and middle class people***?

The answer is that from a direct and open **social** **evil** being imposed on the **bride’s** **side** by the **groom’s** **side**, **dowry** has reinvented itself into becoming:

a) A **desirable** and **vitally** **necessary expense**, not as a **massive** **expense** to provide **financial** **capital** for the **after**-**wedding** period of **married** **life**, but as *a **super**-**extravagance** to be splurged and expended on the actual wedding event(**s**) itself*.

b) And not **necessarily** **always** “**demanded**” by the **groom** or his family (though that could also be a silent and invisible factor), but indulged in by the **bride** and her family out of their own desire to fulfill their *own* dreams of a memorable “**grand** **wedding**” to show the parents’ love for their daughter and/or to maintain their image in society.



Wedding events were *always* a waste of money, *even in the traditional days*, *often sinking the father of a **girl** **child** into the death-trap of poverty and debt*. The typical expenses (*not counting various types of actual ***dowry****) in a traditional wedding in most parts of India, for the bride’s family, would include (starting with the printing and distribution of the **invitation-cards**) things like the **marriage**-**hall**-**rent** and **venue** **decorations**, **accommodation** **for** **guests** coming from distant areas, **refreshments** (breakfast, lunch, etc.) at the venue, the **bride’s** **outfit** (dress, make-up arrangements, etc.), increasingly **photograph**(-**video**) arrangements, and many other things (e.g. **shehnai** players, **flowers**-**and**-**scent** for the arriving guests, etc.), apart from **gifts** and **presentations** (“**aaher**”,
“**maan**-**paan**”, etc.) for **close** **relatives** from both sides and for the **officiating** **priests**.

But, starting perhaps with **films** and **serials** (like “**Ham Aapke Hain Kaun**” and “**Saas Bhi kabhi Bahu Thi**”), and very actively and increasingly propagated thereafter by commercial businesses and media (films, serials, advertisements), **weddings** turned into **mega**-**events**. Apart from the actual **wedding** **day** **programs** (the cost of which could be multiplied several times over by having “**destination weddings**” in exotic places), there used to be relatively minor **religious** **ceremonies** even earlier. But now completely new special **mega**-**events** on different days (“**haldi**”, “**mehndi**”, “**sangeet**”, etc.) started become the “**in**-**thing**” all over the country. Almost every one of these special **mega**-**events** had all the same or similar paraphernalia (**special** **invitation** **cards**, **venue expenses**, **expensive** **clothes** and **food**, etc.): and consequently *the cost of “marrying off” a daughter has become countless times more **expensive** and **money**-**incinerating** than ever before*.

And the main point to be noted is that *in the case of most of these “**events**”, it is the **bride** herself (and not necessarily also the **groom** or his **family**) who craves all these replications of **wasteful filmy extravaganzas***.

Note that **none of this is classified as part of the **social** **evil** that is “**dowry**” which leads to **daughters** becoming a **financial** **burden** on the family of their birth**. But in effect it is *exactly* *that*: *imagine the plight of **families** **with** **daughters** (with emphasis on the plural form)*.

**Empowering the Breaking-India-Brigades to Hijack Issues of Justice**



**Shrikant G. Talageri**

  

The hot news at the moment (today is 26 July 2026) is the resignation of the Central Education Minister, Dharmendra Pradhan, after the CJP (“Cockroach Janata Party”) agitation in Delhi (and elsewhere).

As I stopped reading newspapers and watching news programs after the 2019 Lok Sabha elections, these “hot” issues come to my attention only when some bhakts bring them to my attention. This time, I heard a great many bhakts, friends of mine, claiming that the whole agitation was controlled and manipulated by the leftists and wokes and other Breaking-India forces as a reaction to the fact that Dharmendra Pradhan had finally initiated a cleaning up process in Indian text-books in matters of leftist-controlled anti-Hindu and anti-Indian distortions of Indian history (among other subjects) which have completely dominated textbook-writing in India in the last six decades. The agitation, I was told, was intended to put a full stop to this cleaning up process. \[There is of course that other section of bhakts who support whatever the BJP does, which does not bother with discussing pros and cons, or with condemning the leftists or defending Dharmendra Pradhan\].

I am not the final authority on whether this claim is true or not. It is possible that, if you scratch them, the leaders of the CJP will turn out to be Breaking-India moles. But I at least (at the moment, until more concrete evidence surfaces, or the leaders of the CJP openly show their “true faces” in full) could not find any evidence for this (beyond the fact that all the opposition leaders have jumped into the fray to support the CJP agitation, and that the media is broadcasting anti-Hindu, anti-Indian and woke comments, slogans and views from people participating in the agitation – *but then such people will join up in any agitation where they could try to insert their own agendas into the picture*).

So far, while opinions may differ, and one can have reservations on some of the points above, none of those points seem to me at least to represent Breaking-India or anti-Hindu or anti-India agendas *or to represent any kind of interference in the cleaning up process of the hitherto woke-dominated and anti-Hindu Indian education system*. *If* and *when* the CJP shows that *that* is its real agenda, then will be the time to deal with the menace.

To point out the simplest and most relevant aspect of the matter: it would be a very simple matter to simply replace Dharmendra Pradhan with a new minister who would be *even* *more* *determinedly* *committed* to take forward the “**clean up the Education System from the clutches of the woke leftist Breaking India forces**” program. That Dharmendra Pradhan was particularly committed to the program is not, in any case, demonstrated by the way in which Michel Danino was ousted from the NCERT social science textbook committee in March 2026: Danino himself, in an interview with the Indian Express, said “**NCERT threw us under the bus**” – shades of Noopur Sharma!

\[Incidentally, I am not generally in favor of the fake policy of ministers resigning and running away from the task instead of facing up to their duties and taking stringent actions and measures to rectify matters. 1. 2. This in turn provides fig-leaf excuses to enable the BJP to unobtrusively slide out of the alleged “Hindu agenda” being carried out by stray individuals within the party or government. In short: the big question is, what is going to happen now to the process of cleaning up the education system from the clutches of the woke leftist Breaking India Forces – assuming such a process was ever really in place.

She and her husband were physically beaten, and she was gang-raped, in retaliation. But the judge in the trial court acquitted all the accused on the ground that they were all members of upper castes and the judge could not accept that upper caste people could have defiled themselves by physically molesting a lower-caste woman!! The accused escaped without any punishment, and their acquittal was celebrated in a big rally of their caste brethren in which the local BJP MLAs were present. The point is that all this happened in 1992-1993 at a time when the Ayodhya movement was in full flow. The issue of Bhanwari Devi had no importance at all in the eyes of the BJP or the Sangh Parivar. All such issues were just to be ignored\!

Apart from other matters of right and wrong, the point here is that the so-called “**Hindutva**” ideology of India’s Hindu parties is pure **Rightism** and not **Hindutva**. And it is not just **Hindu**/**Hindutva** issues which are irrelevant to them in their insatiable hunger for votes and power: issues of **socio**-**economic** **justice** are even more irrelevant. The difference between the two, for them, is that **Hindutva** issues can be used, in election times, to drum up votes and support; whereas **genuine** **issues** of **socio**-**economic** **justice** (*as *opposed* to* **socio**-**economic** **policies** of **caste**-**based** **reservations**, which are **claimed** to promote **socio**-**economic** **justice**, regardless of whether they are actually just or not, *and which can also bring in votes and support*) are to be completely shunned.

Therefore it should not surprise if, along with people who genuinely do care for issues of socio-economic justice or of corruption or environment, it is people with Breaking-India agendas or plain electoral-political-agendas who manage to take up vantage positions as champions of right versus wrong whenever such issues arise.

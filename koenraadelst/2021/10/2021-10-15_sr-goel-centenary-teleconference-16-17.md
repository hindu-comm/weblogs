SR Goel Centenary Conference 16-17 October 2021

IndiaFacts presents:

  

SITARAM GOEL CENTENARY CONFERENCE

  

16th - 17th October \| 9.30 AM - 6 PM IST

  

Opening session on the life and significance of Sri SR Goel \| 9.30 to 11.00 am

  

1\. Koenraad Elst: Intro situating SR Goel’s work

2\. Harish Chandra: SR Goel as I knew him

3\. Shrikant Talageri: My memories of SR Goel

  

Session 2 on the literary aspect \| 11.00am to 1.00pm

  

1\. Dileep Karanth: SR Goel as a novelist

2\. Saradindu Mukherji: SR Goel and the Bengali writers Rabindranath Tagore and SC Chatterjee

  

Session 3 on Ram Swarup, 2 pm to 4 pm

  

1\. Ramakrishnan Sitaraman: Ram Swarup’s view of God and neighbour in the light of the Mosaic

distinction

2\. Koenraad Elst: The Changers’ Club’s first publication: Ram Swarup’s \*Indictment\* (of the

Quit India leadership)

  

Session 4 on the challenge of Christianity, 4pm to 6 pm

  

Marc Joris: A second look at SR Goel's account of colonial Christianity, Mahatma Gandhi and Hindu strategic shortcomings

Pankaj Saxena: Does secularization lead to christianization?

  

  

Sunday 17 October 2021

  

Session 5 on intra-Hindu controversies \| 9.30 to 11.00 am

  

1\. Shankar Sharan: SR Goel and the Sangh

2\. Ranbir Sekhon: Voice of India in practice: Hindu Human Rights

  

  

Session 6 on the challenge of Islam \| 11.00am to 1.00pm

  

1\. Jijith Nadumuri Ravi: SR Goel on the backdrop of the Moplah riots

2\. Ramakrishnan Sitaraman: Sita Ram Goel’s Conclusions on Iconoclasm and Theology in the Context of Non-Indian history

  

Koenraad Elst: In conclusion \| 1.00pm to 1.30pm

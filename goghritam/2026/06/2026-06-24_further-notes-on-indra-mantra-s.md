devAmR^ita-varShiNI – देवामृत वर्षिणी

## Further notes on indramantra-s

[![](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/gemini_generated_image_7wlwml7wlwml7wlw.png?w=535)](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/gemini_generated_image_7wlwml7wlwml7wlw.png)

Continuing with taking note of indra mantra-s outside the regular shruti [like earlier](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/2025/11/17/indra-upasana-outside-the-realm-of-the-shruti/), two more will be made note of below, from various Telugu sources. All three procedures were published in Telugu and the first one possibly in Kannada and Tamil too. It is unknown if these vidhi-s are popular outside these three states. Material is presented as is without any attempt to correct.

[![](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image.png?w=296)](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image.png)

indra yantra

indra upAsanA kramam: The procedure for the ritual/worship of Indra.

Waking up early in the morning, reaching the riverbank, dropping a silver coin or a copper coin into the river, clapping hands and saying: “O Goddess Ganga, awake from your sleep! O Goddess Bhagirathi, awake from your sleep! O Resident of Vishnu’s feet, awake from your sleep! Grant me your vision, protect me, wash away my sins, come Ganga, arise Ganga!”

Saying this three times, stepping into the river facing east and bathing as prescribed earlier, one should return home in the same wet clothes. Applying sandalwood paste as an Urdhva puNDra, one should perform japa with a rosary made of nine gems. While a little unclear, it seems to suggest that one should wear a nava ratna mAlA and also perform japa with the same.

The Order of Rituals / Prescribed Rules.

Wrapping a white cotton thread around a copper pot (*Kalasha*), and inside it invoking ga~NgA, arranging mango leaves, placing a coconut on top, and applying turmeric and vermilion, decorating it with flowers, and offering incense (*Dhupa*) and a lighted lamp, perform abhiShekaM for this yantra with perfume, civet oil (*punugu*), *javvadi* (fragrant paste), green camphor (*pacha karpuram*) saffron, and rose water, and wrap it in a white silk cloth, having images/figures of a white elephant and a white horse drawn/crafted, and on those figures, place the yantra and perform laM iti pa~ncha pUja. gaNesha must also be worshipped.

Meditation on the Guru (*Guru Dhyanam*)

Making a ball of turmeric paste and placing it in front of the Yantra,

These lines contain a list of continuous short mantras dedicated to various aspects of the Guru:

ॐ देवगुरु \| ॐ विद्यागुरु ॐ देवेन्द्रगुरु \| ॐ विमलगुरु ॐ सुरगुरु \| ॐ अचलगुरु ॐ मोक्षगुरु \|
ॐ आदिगुरु \| ॐ अनादिगुरु

*oṃ devaguru \| oṃ vidyāguru oṃ devendraguru \| oṃ vimalaguru oṃ suraguru \| oṃ acalaguru oṃ mokṣaguru \| oṃ ādiguru \| oṃ anādiguru*

ॐ बृहस्पते मम रक्ष रक्ष मम कार्यसिद्धिं देहि देहि इन्द्रराज वशमानय आनय, इन्द्रभोगं वशमानय आनय, इन्द्रलोकं वशमानय आनय, गजतुरग आन्दोलिकादि धन कनकवस्तुवाकर्षय आकर्षय, मां रक्ष रक्ष स्वाहा।

*oṃ bṛhaspate mama rakṣa rakṣa mama kāryasiddhiṃ dehi dehi indrarāja vaśamānaya ānaya, indrabhogaṃ vaśamānaya ānaya, indralokaṃ vaśamānaya ānaya, gajaturaga āndoḷikādi dhana kanakavastuvākarṣaya ākarṣaya, māṃ rakṣa rakṣa svāhā.*

Om, O Brihaspati, protect me, protect me! Grant fulfilment to my endeavours, grant it! Bring the King of Gods (Indra) under control, bring him! Bring the enjoyments of Indra under control, bring them! Bring the realm of Indra under control, bring it! Attract elephants, horses, palanquins, wealth, gold, and material objects, attract them! Protect me, protect me, Svaha!

Perform laM iti pa~ncha pUja

गुरुब्रह्मं गुरोर्विष्णुं गुरुदेवोमहेश्वरः । गुरुसाक्षात्परब्रह्मं तस्मैश्रीगुरुवेनमः॥

*gurubrahmaṃ gurorviṣṇuṃ gurudevomaheśvaraḥ \|* *gurusākṣātparabrahmaṃ tasmaiśrīguruvenamaḥ.*

Performing circumambulations and salutations and utter the sankalpa

**Sankalpa:**

एवंगुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभतिथौ अस्माकं सहकुटुम्बानां क्षेमस्थैर्यविजय आयुरारोग्य धनकनकवस्तुवाहनान्दोलिका फलप्राप्तिसिद्ध्यर्थं जपे विनियोगः॥

*evaṃguṇa viśeṣaṇa viśiṣṭāyāṃ śubhatithau asmākaṃ sahakuṭumbānāṃ kṣemasthairyavijaya āyurārogya dhanakanakavastuvāhānāndoḷikā phalaprāptisiddhyarthaṃ jape viniyogaḥ.*

As mentioned previously, Pranayama must be performed for the Yantra.

अस्य श्रीइन्द्रदेवता बृहस्पतिऋषिः, गायत्री छन्दः, ऐं श्रीं बीजं ह्रीं क्लीं कीलकं आं क्रों शक्तिः मम इष्टदेवता इन्द्रदेवता प्रीत्यर्थं जपं करिष्ये।

*asya śrīindradevatā bṛhaspatirṛṣiḥ, gāyatrī chandaḥ, aiṃ śrīṃ bījaṃ hrīṃ klīṃ kīlakaṃ āṃ kroṃ shaktiH mama iṣṭadevatā indradevatā prītyarthaṃ japaṃ kariṣye.*

laM iti pa~ncha pUja

ॐ इन्दिराय विद्महे ॐ श्रीं राजाय धीमहि तन्नो इन्द्रः प्रचोदयात्।

*oṃ indirāya vidmahe oṃ śrīṃ rājāya dhīmahi tanno indraḥ pracodayāt.*

मन्त्रराजाय विद्महे महामन्त्राय धीमहि तन्नो मन्त्रः प्रचोदयात्।

*mantrarājāya vidmahe mahāmantrāya dhīmahi tanno mantraḥ pracodayāt.*

यन्त्रराजाय विद्महे महायन्त्राय धीमहि तन्नो इन्द्रः प्रचोदयात्।

*yantrarājāya vidmahe mahāyantrāya dhīmahi tanno indraḥ pracodayāt.*

ॐ श्रीदेवं इन्द्रराजं श्वेतगजारूढम् ।  
वज्रायुधकरं देवं ऐरावतगजप्रियम् ।  
दीर्घकालमायुष्मन्तं देदीप्यप्रभाकरम् ।  
रत्नसिंहासनं धीरं अष्टैश्वर्यप्रभुं भजे ॥

*oṃ śrīdevaṃ indrarājaṃ śvetagajārūḍham \|* *vajrāyudhakaraṃ devaṃ airāvatagajapriyam \|* *dīrghakālamāyuṣmantaṃ dedīpyaprabhākaram \|* *ratnasiṃhāsanaṃ dhīraṃ aṣṭaiśvaryaprabhuṃ bhaje.*

Om, I worship the glorious divine King Indra, who is mounted upon a white elephant, who holds the thunderbolt (*Vajra*) weapon in his hand, the deity who is fond of his elephant Airavata, who possesses an immensely long life, who shines with intense brilliance, who sits courageously upon a gem-studded throne, and who radiates the eight kinds of wealth and prosperity.

**Moola Mantra**

ॐ सुरलोकनाथाय, सुरकोटिनायकं, देवेन्द्राय, आं क्रों श्रीं, ऐं क्लीं श्रीं, वं वं शं शं ह्रीं ह्रीं नमः स्वाहा।

*oṃ suralokanāthāya, surakoṭināyakaṃ, devendrāya, āṃ kroṃ śrīṃ, aiṃ klīṃ śrīṃ, vaṃ vaṃ śaṃ śaṃ hrīm hrīṃ namaḥ svāhā.*

Om, to the Lord of the celestial realm, the leader of millions of gods, to Devendra; *Aam Kroam Shreem, Aim Kleem Shreem, Vam Vam Sham Sham Hreem Hreem*, Salutations, Svaha!

This mantra must be chanted every day, in the morning and evening sessions to a count of 1108 times, for a duration of 108 days. For those who perform the chanting in this manner, all forms of poverty will be destroyed, and they will live adorned with all ornaments, possessing boundless wealth and prosperity.

वं वं वरुणराजं वशमानय वशमानय। शं शं वायुराजं नभोनभसहितम्। सं सं सकलभोगं सदमलामोदम्। हं हं हंसरूपं आगच्छ आगच्छ॥

*vaṃ vaṃ varuṇarājaṃ vaśamānaya vaśamānaya \|* *śaṃ śaṃ vāyurājaṃ nabhonabhasahitam \|* *saṃ saṃ sakalabhogaṃ sadamalāmodam \|* *haṃ haṃ haṃsarūpaṃ āgaccha āgaccha.*

ऐरावतगजारूढं उच्चैःश्रवमश्वारूढम्। सप्तमेघरथारूढं नर्तकन्याप्रमोदितम्। राजसन्तामसप्रौढं राजराजविराजितम्। अष्टैश्वर्यमहाभोगं आनन्दं परिचारितम्॥

*airāvatagajārūḍhaṃ uccaiḥśravamaśvārūḍham \|* *saptamegharathārūḍhaṃ nartakanyāpramoditam \|* *rājasantāmasaprauḍhaṃ rājarājavirājitam \|* *aṣṭaiśvaryamahābhogaṃ ānandaṃ paricāritam.*

He who is mounted upon the elephant Airavata, who rides the divine horse Uchchaihshravas, who rides a chariot drawn by the seven clouds, who is delighted by dancing maidens (apsaras), who is majestic amid majestic royal assemblies, who shines as the king of kings, who commands the great enjoyment of the eight forms of wealth, and who is surrounded by pure bliss. In this manner, one must perform the ritual daily without fail.

Other indra and indrANI prayoga-s: shatru vashya kara indra prayoga:

“Oṁ jāṁ jīṁ jūṁ jēṁ jaiṁ jauṁ jaḥ laṁ mahēndrāya svāhā \|\|”

“ॐ जां जीं जूं जें जैं जौं जः लं महेन्द्राय स्वाहा ॥”

Having recited this mantra one lakh (100,000) times, if a sacrificial fire ritual (*homam*) is performed ten thousand times using the sacrificial wood twigs (*samidhas*) of guruviMda (Rosary Pea / *Abrus precatorius*) and arka plant/jilleDu (Calotropis / *Calotropis gigantea*), even the most powerful enemy will beg for surrender and remain subdued.

strI vashya prayoga with indrANI mantra:

“Oṁ namo śacī indrāṇī drīṁ drīṁ klīṁ svāhā \|\|”

“ॐ नमो शची इन्द्राणी द्रीं द्रीं क्लीं स्वाहा ॥”

To be chanted one lakh (100,000) times near a riverbank, for siddhi. After that, by uttering the *gotra and* name of the desired woman and chanting it 1008 times, that woman will come under the sAdhaka’s influence/attraction.

devAmR^ita-varShiNI – देवामृत वर्षिणी

## anga devatas of shUlinI durgA –mahAvidyA

[![](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/gemini_generated_image_rmu8nyrmu8nyrmu8_mod.png?w=977)](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/gemini_generated_image_rmu8nyrmu8nyrmu8_mod.png)

Given that, [few mantrins’ view of the deity is inaccurate](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/2025/05/21/pratikriya-sankalpam/), we will explore the a~Nga vidya-s of this [mahAvidyA](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/2025/09/28/the-vaishnava-and-shaiva-reflections-of-salva-raja-bhairavi/).

From the old Kamakoti Mandali post, beyond the ten, there exists list of 13 and 16 mahAvidyA-s.

“An alternate list consists of thirteen Mahāvidyās:1. Kālī 2. Tārā 3. Tripurasundarī 4. Bhuvaneśvarī 5. Chinnamastā 6. Tripurabhairavī 7. Bagalāmukhī 8. Mātaṅgī 9. Dhūmāvatī 10. Kamalātmikā 11. Chaṇḍayogīśvarī 12. Laghuśyāmā 13. Tripuṭā”

“In the context of Srividya, sixteen Mahāvidyās are listed: 1. Kālī 2. Tārā 3. Tripurasundarī 4. Bhuvaneśvarī 5. Chinnamastā 6. Tripurabhairavī 7. Bagalāmukhī 8. Mātaṅgī 9. Dhūmāvatī 10. Kamalātmikā 11. Vanadurgā 12. Shūlinī 13. Ashvārūḍhā 14. Trilokyavijayā 15. Mahāvārāhī 16. Annapūrṇā”

This can be looked at in two ways. One is prAmANika and direct and the second is indirect and derivative but yet based on accepted principles/suggestions from authentic mantra shAstra sources.

The derivative one: This is usually said in the context of prayoga-s and even though is it not mentioned about a~Nga devata-s, the principle that there is no difference between these deities is being applied. The general suggestion is that whatever prayoga-s are mentioned for vanadurgA they can be applied to shUlinI durgA also. So the [a~Nga mantra-s defined for vanadurgA](https://goghritam.wordpress.com/2026/08/12/vanadurga-navanga/) apply for shUlinI as well which includes sharabha and kAlI. There is a reasonable overlap in the lists of a~Nga vidya-s. It is interesting that each list includes one of the two notorious mahAvidyA-s from the group of ten.

कुर्यात्प्रयोगानपि दावदुर्गाकल्पोदितान् वै मनुनाऽमुना च ।  
मन्त्री जपार्चाहुततर्पणान्तान्नाल्पो हि मन्त्रोरनयोर्विशेषः ॥

The practitioner (*mantrī*) should indeed perform the ritual applications (*prayōga*) prescribed in the *Dāva-Durgā Kalpa* (i.e. vana durgA kalpa) using either this (shUlinI durgA) mantra or that one, completing them through the sequence of Japa, Arcā, Homa, and Tarpaṇa. Indeed, there is not even a small difference between these two mantras

The direct and prAmANika shUlinI a~Nga vidya-s: There are 20 a~Nga vidyA-s for shUlinI and are given as below as per the shUlinI mantra kosha:

प्रत्यङ्गिरा भद्रकाली शरभेशोऽथोग्रभैरवः।

बीरभद्रो मन्मथश्च भाषा लक्ष्मीः पुलिन्दिनी॥१॥

महाशास्ता धूम्रिना च बड़वानलभैरवः।

बटुकश्च षण्मुखश्च कालो गणपतिस्तथा॥२॥

अष्टौ च मातृका देव्यौ नृसिंहस्यार्ष्यदेवता।

आकाशभैरवश्चेति शूलिन्या अङ्गदेवताः॥३॥

1\) pratya~NgirA, 2) bhadrakAlI 3) sharabheshvara, 4) ugra bhairava, 5) vIrabhadra, 6) manmatha, 7) bhAShA (sarasvatI), 8) mahAlakShmI, 9) pulindinI. 10) mahAshAstA, 11) dhUmAvatI, 12) baDabAnala bhairava, 13) vaTuka bhairava, 14) shaNmukha, 15) kAla, 16) gaNapati, 17) aShTa mAtR^ikA, 18) narasiMha, 19) Ashu tArkShya/garuDa, (ArShya devata – explanation is given as referring to Ashu garuDa) and 20) AkAsha bhairava.

Which is the particular bhadrakAlI being referred to here among the 72 types of bhadrakAli-s? It can be seen that the deities listed out here are primarily discussed in the AkAsha bhairava kalpa. Some of the deities appear as a sequence one after the other in the AkAsha bhairava kalpa and even the usage bhAShA for sarasvatI is from the said text. The specific bhadrakAlI spoken of here is the 16 handed bhadrakAlI and her mantra from the AkAsha bhairava kalpa. ugra bhairava: This is said to be a reference to a form of sharabheshvara. There seem to be ugra bhairava mantra-s in vogue which refer to him explicitly as sharabheshvara. Like the dashamayI bAlA stotraM, there is also dasha mahAvidyA mayi shUlinI durgA stotraM where all dasha mahAvidyA-s are seen as forms of shUlinI durgA.

Kerala traditions have a deployment of shUlinI durgA where she is particularly invoked to neutralize the harm arising from prayoga-s done with 448 mantra mUrti-s, 400 kuTTichAtan-s, 72 bhadrakAli-s, 42 *Venthalas* (vetAla-s?) (fierce spirit forms), 12 types of ghaNtAkarNa-s and evil sorcery arising from those who practice oDi vidya-s (some evil sorcery practiced in Kerala), among other things. The bhadrakAlI and mahAshAsta being shown as a~Nga vidya-s above is not a mere statement, but seems linked to actual practices/deployments and based on real capability in neutralizing related prayoga-s.

The pratya~Nga devata-s for shUlinI durgA are said to be gharmaTikA, chi~nchiNI & markaTikA (who is also an a~Nga vidyA for dhUmAvati). – *With thanks to shrI Deepak for providing the material and proof reading of the transliteration.*

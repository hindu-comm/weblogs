The significance of General Tso’s chicken (and pizza)

Podcaster and Sōtō Zen practitioner Vincent Moore invited me this week to be interviewed on his Buddhist podcast [Paths of Practice](https://www.youtube.com/@PathsofPractice). Naturally we talk a lot about [my upcoming book](https://loveofallwisdom.com/after-anger/), and I think the interview gives you a great taster of what’s in the book, but we cover a lot of other ground. Especially, it’s a very personal interview, covering topics like how I first got into Śāntideva, my daily Buddhist practice, my unintentional digital detox in 2005, and the time I decided not to listen to Nine Inch Nails. You can watch it below or at its [original location on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x4lBhTdEEEc).

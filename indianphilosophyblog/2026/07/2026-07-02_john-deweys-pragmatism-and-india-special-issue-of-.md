Postdoctoral Fellowship in Jain Studies

Here is a message from Scott Stroud (University of Texas at Austin) about [a new special issue of the journal *Dewey Studies* focused on John Dewey’s pragmatism and India.](https://opensiuc.lib.siu.edu/dewey-studies/vol9/iss1/)

> The first special issue of a journal dedicated to exploring John
> Dewey, pragmatism, and India has been released. The journal is “Dewey
> Studies,” a journal of the John Dewey Society. It features a range of
> interesting pieces from scholars in India and south Asia and the west
> exploring a range of topics—Ambedkar, Phule, Vivekananda, Iqbal,
> education, democracy, caste, feminism, and more. The open access issue
> can be found
> here:**<https://opensiuc.lib.siu.edu/dewey-studies/vol9/iss1/>**

Is delusion a modern institution?

Prof. Anjana Jacob (Georgetown University at Qatar) reached out to me, asking to share [a job ad for an open rank position](https://philjobs.org/job/show/31969). Some readers of this blog may be interested given the range of specializations mentioned in the ad:

> The successful candidate will hold a Ph.D. in philosophy, or a closely
> related field, and will have a research focus in one or more of the
> following areas: non-Western philosophy (especially Islamic and Arabic
> philosophy, African/Africana philosophy, Asian philosophy),
> Continental philosophy, philosophy of science (broadly construed),
> philosophy of social science, or applied ethics. They will contribute
> to the foundational and upper-level offerings of the Culture and
> Politics (CULP) major and to the BSFS core curriculum and must be able
> to teach a course in Political and Social Thought.

**The deadline to apply is Oct. 15, 2026.** For more information and instructions for how to apply, see [the full job ad on PhilJobs.](https://philjobs.org/job/show/31969)

Please reach out to Prof. Jacob with any questions you may have (email in [the job ad](https://philjobs.org/job/show/31969)).
